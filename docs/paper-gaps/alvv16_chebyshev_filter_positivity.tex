For $a<b<u$, define
\begin{align}
    f(x)&=\frac{u+b-2x}{u-b},\notag\\
    p_n(x)&=\frac{T_n(f(x))}{T_n(f(a))}.
    \label{eq:alvv_chebyshev_normalization}
\end{align}
The denominator is positive. The polynomial satisfies $p_n(a)=1$, is at
least one for $x\le a$, and for every $x\in[b,u]$ obeys
\begin{align}
    |p_n(x)|&\le2\exp\left(-2n\sqrt{\frac{b-a}{u-a}}\right).
    \label{eq:alvv_chebyshev_contraction}
\end{align}
These estimates do not imply nonnegativity on $[b,u]$.
For $a=0$, $b=1/2$, $u=1$ and $n=1$,
\begin{align}
    p_1(x)&=\frac{3-4x}{3},\notag\\
    p_1(\operatorname{diag}(0,1))&=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1/3).\notag
\end{align}
The quadratic form at the second basis vector is $-1/3$, so this filter
is not positive semidefinite although the Hamiltonian is positive semidefinite.
Even degree alone does not repair the issue: $T_2(0)=-1$.

The normalized construction occurs in Arad--Kitaev--Landau--Vazirani,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1162}{arXiv:1301.1162v1}, source lines 541--583,
and in Arad--Landau--Vazirani--Vidick,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08828}{arXiv:1602.08828v2}, Definition
\texttt{def:Cheby-AGSP}, source lines 789--803. The latter paper's Definition
\texttt{def:spectral-AGSP}, lines 570--581, requires positivity, whereas
Theorem \texttt{thm:Cheby-AGSP} identifies the unsquared filter with such
an operator. The sign computation contradicts that additional clause.

The Hermitian spectral conclusions remain valid. For a Hermitian $H$ with
spectrum contained in $\{a\}\cup[b,u]$, let $P$ be its spectral projection
at $a$, with arbitrary multiplicity. Then
\begin{align}
    p_n(H)P&=P=Pp_n(H),\notag\\
    \|p_n(H)-P\|&\le2\exp\left(-2n\sqrt{\frac{b-a}{u-a}}\right).\notag
\end{align}
For a general low-energy interval, write $P_-=P_{(-\infty,a]}$.
The conclusion is instead $P_-p_n(H)P_-\ge P_-$.
These are the assertions proved by
\leanid{Matrix.IsHermitian.exists_polynomial_spectral_filter} and
\leanid{Matrix.IsHermitian.exists_polynomial_approximating_isolated_energy_projection}.
The degree-zero and singleton-interval cases are included in the scalar
construction. Squaring the filter restores positivity, but changes degree
and contraction parameters; the statements above concern the unsquared filter.
