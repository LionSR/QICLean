\section{The assertion}

Let $T:\MN{d}\to\MN{d}$ be positive and trace preserving, and assume that its
trace adjoint satisfies the Schwarz inequality. Define the fixed-point space
\begin{align}
    \mathcal F_T
        &=\left\{X\in\MN{d}\mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}T(X)=X\right\}.
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_fixed_point_space}
\end{align}
Let $\rho$ be any maximum-rank density matrix in $\mathcal F_T$, and interpret
$\rho^{-1/2}$ as the inverse square root on the support of $\rho$ and as zero
on its kernel. Corollary~6.7 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} states that
\begin{align}
    \rho^{-1/2}\mathcal F_T\rho^{-1/2}
        &=
        \left\{\rho^{-1/2}X\rho^{-1/2}
            \mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}
            X\in\mathcal F_T\right\}
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_inverse_sandwich_set}
\end{align}
is a $*$-algebra.

The corollary uses the maximal-support property established earlier in
Section~6.4 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}: every fixed point of $T$ has
support and range contained in the support of a maximum-rank fixed point.
Consequently, conjugation by $\rho^{-1/2}$ discards no part of
$\mathcal F_T$.

\section{The formalization}

The source-general implementation is in
\path{QICLean/Channel/FixedPoint/AbstractWeightedFixedPoints.lean}. It assumes
exactly that $T$ is positive and trace preserving and that its trace adjoint is
Schwarz. It assumes neither a Kraus representation nor complete positivity.

For an arbitrary positive semidefinite fixed point $\rho$, let $Q$ be its
support projection. The declaration
\leanid{IsPositiveMap.weightedCornerFixedPointsStarSubalgebra} proves that
\begin{align}
    \mathcal W_{\rho,T}
        &=
        \left\{Y\in Q\MN{d}Q
            \mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}
            T(\sqrt{\rho}\,Y\sqrt{\rho})
                =\sqrt{\rho}\,Y\sqrt{\rho}\right\}
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_weighted_corner_carrier}
\end{align}
is a $*$-subalgebra of $Q\MN{d}Q$.

Its multiplication proof follows the density-block decomposition in
Wolf's Theorem~6.14 \cite{Wolf2012Quantum}. On each support sector, fixed
blocks have the form $\sigma_k\otimes X_k$, while $\rho$ has blocks
$\sigma_k\otimes R_k$, with both density factors positive definite. The
weighted product remains fixed because
\begin{align}
    &(\sigma_k\otimes X_k)
      (\sigma_k^{-1}\otimes R_k^{-1})
      (\sigma_k\otimes Z_k)\nonumber\\
        &\qquad
        =\sigma_k\otimes(X_kR_k^{-1}Z_k).
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_weighted_block_product}
\end{align}
This is the factor-swapped form of the
$\mathcal M_{d_k}\otimes\rho_k$ notation in
Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}, as already recorded for the formalization of
Theorem~6.14.

The maximum-rank step is also source-general. The declaration
\leanid{IsPositiveMap.maximalSupport\_of\_maximalRank} in
\path{QICLean/Channel/FixedPoint/AbstractMaximalRank.lean} accepts any
positive semidefinite fixed point whose rank is at least the rank of every
stationary density. It proves
\begin{align}
    QXQ
        &=X
        \qquad\text{for every }X\in\mathcal F_T.
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_maximal_support_identity}
\end{align}
The proof compares the arbitrary maximum-rank choice with
$T_\infty(\mathbf 1)$; it does not replace the source quantifier by a
canonical witness.

Finally,
\leanid{IsPositiveMap.mem\_weightedCornerFixedPointsStarSubalgebra\_iff\_inverseSandwich}
identifies the carrier
(\ref{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_weighted_corner_carrier}) in
both directions with
(\ref{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_inverse_sandwich_set}). The
support identities are
\begin{align}
    \sqrt{\rho} \rho^{-1/2}
        &=Q,
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_left_support_identity}\\
    \rho^{-1/2}\sqrt{\rho}
        &=Q.
    \label{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_right_support_identity}
\end{align}
They establish the carrier equality without assuming that $\rho$ is
invertible on the ambient space. The generic construction also includes the
zero-support case as the zero corner algebra. The density-matrix hypothesis
in Corollary~6.7 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} has trace one and therefore
excludes that case.

\section{Conclusion}

The former Kraus-only scope restriction is eliminated.
\leanid{IsPositiveMap.wolfCorollary67} states Corollary~6.7 of
Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} for a positive trace-preserving linear map with
Schwarz trace adjoint and for every maximum-rank stationary density matrix.
It returns a $*$-algebra whose carrier is exactly the inverse-square-root set
(\ref{eq:wolf_cor67_maximal_support_restriction_inverse_sandwich_set}). The
older declarations in the \leanid{Kraus} namespace remain compatibility
specializations. The maximum-rank and surjectivity declarations delegate to
the source-general proofs rather than providing a separate route.
