\section{Source and completed equivalences}
Wolf's Theorem~8.4 concerns arbitrary vectors $x,y\in\mathbb R^d$.
The local source is
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch08_distance_measures.tex}, lines~220--251,
including Equation~(8.16) \cite{Wolf2012Quantum}.
The declarations in \doclink{QICLean/Analysis/VectorMajorization} prove the
equivalence of decreasing-prefix majorization with equal total sums,
the existence of a doubly stochastic matrix $M$ satisfying $x=My$,
and comparison of the sums of every convex function
$f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$. These are clauses~1, 2 and~4.
There is no positivity, probability normalization, or positive-dimension
assumption on these equivalences.

\section{Remaining characterization}
Clause~3 requires a real orthogonal matrix $O$ such that
$x_i=\sum_j O_{ij}^2y_j$.
It is not supplied by the doubly stochastic characterization: a
particular doubly stochastic matrix need not be orthostochastic.
A proof must construct such an orthogonal matrix for the given vectors,
for example through the real Schur--Horn theorem or successive plane rotations.
This component remains open under \ghissue{47}.
The separate submajorization theorem is also outside the equivalences above.
The containing four-clause theorem and issue are not complete.
