\section{The source hypothesis}

Proposition~7.4 of Ref.~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} (\path{06-transport.tex},
lines~377--434) concerns band metrics
$A(P,Y,F)=(W_P^{-1}W_F^{-1}W_Y)^2$ on the symmetric subspace
$\mathcal S_k=\mathrm{Sym}^k\mathcal V$ (lines~251--258). Its commutation
condition (lines~273--276) asks that every old or new metric of band $g$
commute with every old or new metric of band $g'\neq g$. Since the metrics
are operators on $\mathcal S_k$, this is commutation on $\mathcal S_k$. The
paper uses this point when it applies the proposition: under the nesting
condition of \path{07-comparators.tex} (lines~26--35), the different bands'
metric algebras commute only after complementary labels are identified on the
symmetric space, using $W_{Q,k}=W_{Q^c,k}$ on $\mathcal S_k$
(\path{05-replicas.tex}, line~312).

\section{The former formal restriction}

The formal band metric is the operator
$(W_P^{-1}W_F^{-1}W_Y)^2$ on the full tensor power
$\mathcal V^{\otimes k}$. It commutes with the copy permutations, so it
preserves $\mathcal S_k$ and restricts there to the source metric. The first
formal cross-band hypothesis asked for commutation of these operators on all of
$\mathcal V^{\otimes k}$, for every $k$.

Commutation on the full tensor power implies commutation on $\mathcal S_k$,
but the converse fails. Central label functions of two subsystems $Q,Q'$
commute on $\mathcal V^{\otimes k}$ when $Q\subseteq Q'$ or $Q\cap Q'=\emptyset$,
but in general not when the two subsystems overlap without nesting. For three
qubits $a,b,c$, $k=3$, $t=1/10$ and the label weights of
\path{05-replicas.tex} (display \texttt{replicas:w-definition}, padding
dimension $8$), a direct computation gives
$\|[W_{ab},W_{bc}]\|_2\approx 3.9\times10^{-4}$ on $\mathcal V^{\otimes 3}$,
while the commutator vanishes on $\mathcal S_3$, where
$W_{ab}=W_c$ and $W_{bc}=W_a$.

In the comparator setting, $P_g=CXU_g$, $F_g=RV_g$, and for $g<g'$ the nesting
condition gives $XU_gY_g\subseteq XU_{g'}$. Every pair of factors from the two
bands is nested or disjoint, except $F_g=RV_g$ and $P_{g'}=CXU_{g'}$, which
overlap in $V_g\cap U_{g'}$ without nesting. The remaining factors of each
band commute with every factor of the other band, so the full-space
commutation of $A_g$ and $A_{g'}$ reduces to that of $W_{RV_g}$ and
$W_{CXU_{g'}}$, which fails in general. The first formal theorem therefore proved
Proposition~7.4 under a commutation hypothesis that the source does not
impose and that its multi-band application need not satisfy.

\section{Resolution}

Let $\Pi$ be the projection onto $\mathcal S_k$. Each band metric $A$ commutes
with $\Pi$, and the mean trees now take as inputs the products over bands of
the operators
\[
  \widetilde A=\Pi A+(I-\Pi),
\]
which equal $A$ on $\mathcal S_k$ and the identity on its orthogonal
complement.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{TensorPower.ReplicaTransport.symBandMetric} and the restated
\leanid{TensorPower.ReplicaTransport.TransportData.CrossBandCommute} in
\path{QICLean/Representation/ReplicaTransport/Setup.lean}, with the properties below
in \path{QICLean/Representation/ReplicaTransport/SymmetricMetric.lean}.}
The cross-band hypothesis is now the source condition: $AA'w=A'Aw$ for every
$w\in\mathcal S_k$ and all metrics $A,A'$ of different bands. The following
facts make the transport argument go through unchanged.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\widetilde A$ is positive definite and commutes with the copy
  permutations.
\item $\widetilde A\widetilde B=\Pi AB+(I-\Pi)$, so $\widetilde A$ and
  $\widetilde B$ commute on $\mathcal V^{\otimes k}$ exactly when
  $\Pi AB=\Pi BA$, that is, when $A$ and $B$ commute on $\mathcal S_k$.
\item For a commuting projection, functional calculus acts block by block:
  $f(\Pi Y+(I-\Pi)Z)=\Pi f(Y)+(I-\Pi)f(Z)$. The proof interpolates $f$ by one
  polynomial on the two finite spectra. Hence
  $\widetilde A^{-1/2}\widetilde B\widetilde A^{-1/2}
   =\Pi A^{-1/2}BA^{-1/2}+(I-\Pi)$, and the relative coherent pin, whose
  lower bound $P_{\theta,k}$ satisfies $\Pi P_{\theta,k}\Pi=P_{\theta,k}$,
  transfers to the extended metrics.
\item For $X$ commuting with $\Pi$, the skew squares of $\widetilde A$ and of
  $A$ agree after multiplication by $\Pi$, so they have the same expectation in
  every density matrix on $\mathcal S_k$. The skew bound of the source
  therefore applies at a split leaf.
\end{enumerate}
On $\mathcal S_k$ the roots, the filtered vectors and the transport states are
those of the source, since every input preserves $\mathcal S_k$ and agrees there
with the source's metric.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Verdict: resolved.} The transport estimate is formalized with the
source's cross-band commutation condition on $\mathcal S_k$. Commutation on
the full tensor power is no longer assumed.
