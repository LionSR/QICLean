\gapnote{local-correction}{resolved}

The proof of Lemma \texttt{l:within} in
\cite[source file \texttt{algo53.tex}, lines 509--515]{Landau2013PolynomialTime}
uses the following inference. A unit witness $v$ has overlap at least
$1-c_\varepsilon/n$ with the unit ground state $\Gamma$, and $\|H\|\le n$;
the proof concludes that its energy is at most $\varepsilon_0+c_\varepsilon$.
The viable-set definition uses unsquared overlap. The direct consequence is
instead $\varepsilon_0+2c_\varepsilon$.

Indeed, put $a=\langle\Gamma,v\rangle$ and write $v=a\Gamma+w$ with
$w\perp\Gamma$. Since $H\Gamma=\varepsilon_0\Gamma$, symmetry gives
\begin{align}
    \langle v,Hv\rangle-\varepsilon_0
     & =\langle w,(H-\varepsilon_0 I)w\rangle
    \le \|H-\varepsilon_0 I\|(1-|a|^2).
    \label{eq:lvv13_witness_energy_overlap}
\end{align}
For the source Hamiltonian, $0\le H\le nI$ and $\varepsilon_0$ is its
ground energy, hence $\|H-\varepsilon_0 I\|\le n$. If
$|a|\ge1-\delta$ and $\delta\ge0$, then
$1-|a|^2=(1-|a|)(1+|a|)\le2\delta$. Applying
(\ref{eq:lvv13_witness_energy_overlap}) with $\delta=c_\varepsilon/n$
gives the corrected excess $2c_\varepsilon$.

The factor cannot be removed from this overlap inference. For
$H=\operatorname{diag}(0,M)$, $\Gamma=(1,0)$ and
$v=(1-\delta,\sqrt{2\delta-\delta^2})$, where $M>0$ and $0<\delta<1$,
the overlap is $1-\delta$ while the excess energy is
$M(2\delta-\delta^2)>M\delta$.

This note corrects only the first energy inference in the proof of
Lemma \texttt{l:within}. The subsequent trimming estimate and the choice
of $c_\varepsilon$ must also account for the corrections in
\url{https://sirui-lu.com/QICLean/paper-gaps/lvv13_trimming_energy.pdf}.
Neither the full lemma nor the viable-set
induction follows from this auxiliary bound alone.
