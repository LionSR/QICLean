\section{The printed statement}

Arad--Landau--Vazirani--Vidick,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08828}{arXiv:1602.08828v2}, trim a viable
subspace cut by cut. Let $S\subseteq\mathcal H_A=\mathcal H_A^1\otimes\cdots
\otimes\mathcal H_A^\ell$ have the orthonormal basis $u_1,\dots,u_s$ and put
$\psi=\sum_iu_i\otimes|i\rangle$. For $j=1,\dots,\ell-1$ the projection
$P^j_{\ge\xi}$ keeps the left Schmidt vectors, across the cut $(j:j+1)$, of
the partially trimmed $\psi$ with Schmidt coefficient at least $\xi$, and
$\operatorname{Trim}_\xi(S)$ is the span of the trimmed $u_i$ (Definition
\texttt{def:trimming}, source lines 642--656). Lemma \texttt{lem:trimming}
(lines 660--697) asserts: if $S$ is $\delta$-viable of dimension $s$ for
$T\subseteq\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B$, and every vector of $T$ has
Schmidt rank at most $b$ across each cut $(j:j+1)$, then
$\operatorname{Trim}_\xi(S)$ is $\delta'$-viable for $T$ with
\begin{align}
    \delta'&\le\delta+\sqrt{\ell bs}\,\xi .
    \label{eq:alvv_trim_printed}
\end{align}

\section{What the argument gives}

The proof fixes a unit $v\in T$ and a unit $u\in S\otimes\mathcal H_B$ with
$|\langle u,v\rangle|^2\ge1-\delta$, and bounds the part of $u$ removed at
each cut by pairing it with $v$. Writing $u=\sum_iu_i\otimes c_i$, the
removed part at the cut $j$ pairs with $v$ as the discarded part of
$\psi$, all of whose Schmidt coefficients are below $\xi$, pairs with
$W=\sum_i\big((I\otimes\langle c_i|)w_j\big)\otimes|i\rangle$, where $w_j$
is the piece of $v$ removed at that cut. The map $w\mapsto W$ acts on the
right of the cut, so $W$ has Schmidt rank at most $b$, not $bs$; and
$\|W\|\le\|u\|\,\|w_j\|$. Hence the removed part at the cut $j$ pairs with
$v$ to at most $\xi\sqrt b\,\|u\|\,\|w_j\|$. The pieces $w_j$ are orthogonal,
because the trimming projections are nested, so
$\sum_j\|w_j\|\le\sqrt{\ell-1}\,\|v\|$ and the trimmed vector $u'$ satisfies
\begin{align}
    |\langle u',v\rangle|&\ge|\langle u,v\rangle|-\varepsilon,
    \qquad\varepsilon=\xi\sqrt{(\ell-1)b}.
    \notag
\end{align}
The source then states that the claimed bound follows. Squaring the
overlap gives instead $(\sqrt{1-\delta}-\varepsilon)^2\ge1-\delta-2\varepsilon$,
that is
\begin{align}
    \delta'&\le\delta+2\sqrt{(\ell-1)b}\,\xi .
    \label{eq:alvv_trim_proved}
\end{align}
The printed bound (\ref{eq:alvv_trim_printed}) does not follow from the
printed argument. The two bounds are not comparable in general: the factor
$s$ disappears and a factor $2$ appears. Whether
(\ref{eq:alvv_trim_printed}) holds is not settled here.

\section{The formal statement}

The formalization proves (\ref{eq:alvv_trim_proved}) for $\xi>0$, with
$\mathcal H_A$ the configurations of a finite set of sites, any nested
family of $m$ cuts in place of the $\ell-1$ cuts of a chain, and the
environment $\mathcal H_B$ on the right of every
cut.\footnote{\leanid{Entropy.isCloseTo_prodExt_trimSubspace} in
    \doclink{QICLean/Analysis/ViableSubspaceTrimming}.} The threshold
$\xi$ is positive in the source, which writes $s\xi^{-2}$. The second
statement of the lemma, that each trimmed basis vector has Schmidt rank at
most $s\xi^{-2}$ across every cut, holds as
printed.\footnote{\leanid{Entropy.schmidtRank_cutVector_trimmed_le}.} Its
running-time clause is not formalized.

\section{Effect on the later arguments}

The lemma is applied in the merge process and in the algorithms (lines 1636,
1670, 1801, 1814 and 1839) with $\xi$ chosen polynomially small, so that the
trimming error is below a fixed constant. A constant factor in the error is
absorbed by rescaling $\xi$ by a constant, which changes the trimmed rank
$s\xi^{-2}$ only by a constant factor, so these uses are unaffected.
