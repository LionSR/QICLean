\title{The Entropy Estimate for an Approximate Ground-Space Projection\\
    as the Shrinking Parameter Tends to Zero}
\author{}
\date{2026-10-22}

\begin{document}
\maketitle
\gapnote{false-source}{open}

The entropy lemma of Arad, Kitaev, Landau and Vazirani states that an approximate
ground-space projection of operator Schmidt rank at most $D$, with squared
shrinking parameter $\Delta$, and a product vector of overlap at least $\mu$
give an entropy bound of the form
\begin{align}
    S & \le C\frac{\log\mu^{-1}}{\log\Delta^{-1}}\log D,
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_printed}
\end{align}
where the $O(\cdot)$ notation in the source is read as an absolute
constant $C$ independent of these parameters.\footnote{The source is
    \cite{Arad2013AreaLaw}, arXiv version~1.
    The local source file \path{1dfrustrated34.tex} labels the lemma
    \path{lem:S} and the equation \path{eq:Sb}, lines~339--348.
    Its approximate ground-space projection is defined at
    \path{def:AGSP}, lines~296--324.}
The estimate (\ref{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_printed}) fails for
strictly positive shrinking parameters, even for a fixed two-site
Hamiltonian with a unique ground state.

Let $\mathcal H=\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^2$ and put
\begin{align}
    \Gamma & =\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2},
           & P                                      & =|\Gamma\rangle\langle\Gamma|,
           & H                                      & =I-P.
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_bell_hamiltonian}
\end{align}
The single two-site term $H$ is a positive contraction. Its ground state is
uniquely $\Gamma$ up to phase, its ground energy is zero, and its spectral
gap is one. For $0<s\le1/\sqrt8$, define
\begin{align}
    K_s & =sI+(1-s)P=I-(1-s)H.
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_agsp_family}
\end{align}
This is a positive contraction satisfying
\begin{align}
    K_s\Gamma & =\Gamma,
              & K_s\psi  & =s\psi\qquad(\psi\perp\Gamma).
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_agsp_action}
\end{align}
Thus the squared shrinking parameter is exactly $\Delta=s^2>0$; it is
attained on every unit vector in the three-dimensional orthogonal
complement of $\Gamma$.

In fact, $K_s$ has operator Schmidt rank $D=4$. To see this, let $E_{ab}$
be the standard matrix units of $M_2(\mathbb C)$. Then
\begin{align}
    P & =\frac12\sum_{a,b=0}^1E_{ab}\otimes E_{ab}.
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_bell_matrix_units}
\end{align}
In the ordered matrix-unit bases $(E_{00},E_{01},E_{10},E_{11})$, the
coefficient matrix of $K_s$ is
\begin{align}
    \frac{1-s}{2}I_4+s vv^{\mathsf T},
    \qquad v=(1,0,0,1)^{\mathsf T}.
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_coefficient_matrix}
\end{align}
It is positive definite, since $(1-s)/2>0$ and $s>0$, and consequently
has rank four. Moreover, $D\Delta=4s^2\le1/2$.

The normalized product vector $|00\rangle$ has overlap $\mu=1/\sqrt2$
with $\Gamma$, while the reduced density matrix is $I_2/2$ and
$S=\log2$. Substituting these values into
(\ref{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_printed}) gives
\begin{align}
    \log2 & \le C\frac{(\log2)^2}{\log(s^{-2})}.
    \label{eq:aklv13_entropy_small_shrinking_entropy_substitution}
\end{align}
Taking $s=2^{-t}$ for an integer $t\ge2$ would require
$1\le C/(2t)$, which is impossible when $t>C/2$. All AGSP parameters
used here are strictly positive, the shrinking bound is attained, and the
ground state is unique.

The printed parameter dependence therefore requires a correction before
it can be used as a uniform entropy estimate. In particular, a bound
with an additive term proportional to $\log D$ is compatible with this
example, whereas the printed quotient alone is not. A corrected estimate
with explicit constants remains to be derived from the Schmidt-weight
tails of the successive approximations.\footnote{This step is tracked in
    \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9836}{TNLean issue \#9836}.
    The finite rank-tail entropy estimates are developed in QICLean.}
This note establishes the counterexample, without claiming a corrected
general entropy theorem. The source's subsequent area-law corollary has
a separate conclusion $S\le O(1)\log D$, which is consistent with the
example.
