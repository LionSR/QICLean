\gapnote{local-correction}{resolved}

The second item of Lemma \texttt{lem:hard-trunc} of
Arad--Landau--Vazirani--Vidick,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08828}{arXiv:1602.08828v2}, source lines
926--948, states that the low-energy subspace $\tilde T_{\eta'}$ of the
truncated Hamiltonian is $\delta$-close to the low-energy subspace $T_\eta$ of
$H$. Its proof (lines 951--969) splits a vector $\psi\in T_\eta$ into pieces
supported on eigenvalues of $H$ in windows of width $2h$, bounds the mass of
each piece above $\varepsilon_0+\eta'$ by Markov's inequality, and sums the
bounds with the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality. Two constants need adjustment;
neither changes the conclusion.

\emph{The number of windows.} Line 963 bounds the number of windows by
$\lceil(\eta-\varepsilon_0)/(2h)\rceil$, which subtracts an energy from a
width. The eigenvalues of $H$ in $T_\eta$ lie in the closed interval
$[\varepsilon_0,\varepsilon_0+\eta]$. Windows $[\varepsilon_0+2hw,
\varepsilon_0+2h(w+1))$ starting at the ground energy cover it with
$N=\lfloor\eta/(2h)\rfloor+1$ windows. This exceeds $\lceil\eta/(2h)\rceil$
by one when $\eta/(2h)$ is an integer, because the interval is closed.

\emph{The gap in Markov's inequality.} The window bound at line 960 is
stated for a centre $\lambda\le\varepsilon_0+\eta$, but the centre of the top
window can lie up to $h$ above $\varepsilon_0+\eta$. For a centre
$\lambda\le\varepsilon_0+\eta+h$ every eigenvalue of $\tilde H_t$ above
$\varepsilon_0+\eta'$ is at distance more than $\eta'-\eta-h$ from
$\lambda$, so the denominator at line 966 is $\eta'-\eta-h$ in place of
$\eta'-\eta$.

With these corrections the proof shows the following. If
$\|(H-\tilde H_t)\varphi\|\le\xi\|\varphi\|$ for $\varphi\in T_\eta$, $h>0$
and $\eta+h<\eta'$, then $\tilde T_{\eta'}$ is $\delta$-close to $T_\eta$
whenever $N(\xi+h)^2\le\delta(\eta'-\eta-h)^2$. For $h=\xi=e^{-\Omega(t-\eta)}$
this holds for $\eta'=\eta+\xi+2\xi\sqrt{N/\delta}$, which has the form
$\eta'=\eta+\sqrt{\eta/\delta}\,e^{-\Omega(t-\eta)}$ of the lemma.

The formal statement is
\leanid{Submodule.isCloseTo_spectralSpanLE_of_perturbation}, stated for two
self-adjoint operators diagonal in given orthonormal bases, with
$\varepsilon_0$ replaced by any lower bound $m$ of the spectrum of $H$ and
$\varepsilon_0+\eta$ by any energy $c$.
