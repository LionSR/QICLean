\section{Zero-error capacity: scope}
Sanz, Pérez-García, Wolf and Cirac, arXiv:0909.5347,
Theorem 2, allows one-shot capacity in its footnote. The formalization
now covers every channel power at or above the Wielandt index, with
unchanged hypotheses. The full-word-span persistence theorem supplies
positive definite outputs at every such power; the strictly positive
trace pairing excludes orthogonal outputs.

The regularized zero-error classical capacity, allowing entangled inputs
across tensor powers, remains outside the formalized definition.
The cited reference [14] is Cubitt, Chen and Harrow,
\emph{Superactivation of the asymptotic zero-error classical capacity
of a quantum channel}, arXiv:0906.2547v2. A future formalization must
define tensor-power coding and prove the corresponding full-rank-output
criterion; the elementary one-shot trace-pairing argument alone must not
be advertised as that stronger result.
