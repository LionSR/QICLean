\section{The source statement}

The cardinality reduction of Landau, Vazirani and Vidick discretizes the
boundary contractions, matrices on $\mathbb C^B\otimes\mathbb C^d$, by a net
in trace norm.\footnote{The source is \cite{Landau2013PolynomialTime},
    version~1 of arXiv:1307.5143, source file \path{algo53.tex}, Lemma
    \path{l:constantbondapprox} at lines~451--460.} The lemma sets
\begin{align}
    \mathcal I_\eta
     & =\{-1,-1+\lfloor\eta/(Bd)^2\rfloor,\dots,1-\lfloor\eta/(Bd)^2\rfloor,1\},
     &
    \mathcal N_\eta
     & =\mathcal I_\eta^{B}\times\mathcal I_\eta^{d},
    \label{eq:lvv13_trace_norm_net_printed}
\end{align}
and states that $\mathcal N_\eta$ has at most
$(2\lceil Bd/\eta\rceil+1)^{2Bd}$ elements and that every $Y$ of trace norm
at most $1$ is within trace-norm distance $\eta$ of some $X\in\mathcal
    N_\delta$. The proof notes that the entries of such a $Y$ have modulus at
most $1$ and that $\|Y-X\|_1\le(Bd)^2\max_{i,j}|Y_{ij}-X_{ij}|$.

\section{The point at issue}

Four slips in the printed statement need reading:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item the step $\lfloor\eta/(Bd)^2\rfloor$ is $0$ for $\eta<(Bd)^2$, so
          it is read as $\eta/(Bd)^2$;
    \item the net is written $\mathcal N_\delta$ for $\mathcal N_\eta$;
    \item the product $\mathcal I_\eta^B\times\mathcal I_\eta^d$ does not
          describe a $(Bd)\times(Bd)$ complex matrix, which has $(Bd)^2$
          complex entries; it is read as a grid for the real and imaginary
          parts of every entry, $2(Bd)^2$ real coordinates in all;
    \item with that grid, of step at most $\eta/(Bd)^2$ on $[-1,1]$, each
          coordinate takes at most $2\lceil(Bd)^2/\eta\rceil+1$ values, so the
          size is at most $(2\lceil(Bd)^2/\eta\rceil+1)^{2(Bd)^2}$, not the
          printed $(2\lceil Bd/\eta\rceil+1)^{2Bd}$.
\end{enumerate}
The distance estimate holds as printed. Rounding the real and imaginary part
of each entry to the grid moves the entry by at most the step, and the trace
norm is at most the sum of the moduli of the $(Bd)^2$ entries.

\section{Effect downstream}

The corrected size is still polynomial in $1/\eta$ for fixed $B$ and $d$.
At $\eta=c_\varepsilon/(2n)$ it is polynomial in $n$, so the cardinality
bound $p_1(n)$ of Claim \path{c:2} (lines~518--519) remains a polynomial, with the
exponent $2(B_{c_\varepsilon}d)^2$ in place of $2B_{c_\varepsilon}d$.

\section{Formalization}

The net is \leanid{Matrix.exists_finset_rectangularTraceNorm_net}, for
square matrices over any finite index type of size $c$: for $\eta>0$ it
gives at most $(2\lceil c^2/\eta\rceil+1)^{2c^2}$ matrices within trace-norm
distance $\eta$ of every matrix of trace norm at most $1$. The two entry
estimates are \leanid{Matrix.norm_apply_le_rectangularTraceNorm} and
\leanid{Matrix.rectangularTraceNorm_le_sum_norm_apply}.
