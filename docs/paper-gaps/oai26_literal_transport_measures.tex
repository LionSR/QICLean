\section{The coherent representation}

The coherent measures in the transport estimate of
Ref.~\cite[Proposition~7.4]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} are measures on the one-copy unit
sphere.\footnote{Source: \path{06-transport.tex}, lines 364--401, displays
\texttt{transport:coherent-measure} and \texttt{transport:states}.}
For a positive matrix $\rho$, the measure is the image of Haar measure with
density $D_k\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P_{Ue_a,k})$ under $U\mapsto Ue_a$.
For a terminal leaf, the Fourier average has density
\begin{align}
 d_j(u,U)&=m_{1/4}(u)D_k
   \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_{j,u}P_{Ue_a,k}).
 \label{eq:literal_transport_density}
\end{align}
This uses the actual terminal state. History and choice weights remain outside
this measure, and its normalized-state mass is $1/2$, not one.

\section{The construction and its hypotheses}

Both measures are defined by weighted pushforward. Their real-integral
identities require positivity of the density; the transported-state identity uses
only admissibility, a nonnegative regulator parameter, and cross-band commutation
on the symmetric subspace. Positivity holds at every real clamped interpolation
parameter. Trace one additionally needs a nonzero supplied vector and nonzero
terminal weight. At zero weight, the accepted normalization of the derivative
map gives zero state and zero measure. The source itself introduces the terminal
states on the open interpolation interval; the inactive boundary assertion is
about this accepted extension, not an extra assertion attributed to the paper.

Absolute integrability of (\ref{eq:literal_transport_density}) is proved by
bounding a continuous function on the product of the replica-space unitary
group and the one-copy unitary group. Both groups are needed. The resulting
bound may depend on the fixed interpolation parameter, replica count, and
supplied vector. No continuity in the interpolation parameter follows from
this argument.

\section{Scope of the representation}

The weighted pushforwards establish the coherent and Fourier integral
representations, their finiteness, the normalized mass on active leaves, and
the zero-state identities under the hypotheses above.\footnote{Formal modules:
\path{QICLean/Representation/ReplicaTransport/CoherentMeasure.lean} and
\path{QICLean/Representation/ReplicaTransport/LeafMeasure.lean}.}

The representation theorem alone does not construct
physical scanner data or a comparator. Specialization to fill and charge data,
identification of entropy and energy symbols, the physical supplied vectors,
and the remaining parameter-continuity and endpoint norm estimates are separate
steps. In particular, including zero replicas for an explicitly supplied nonzero
scalar vector says nothing about a physical ground-state energy identity at zero
replicas. The note records no defect in the source mathematics.
