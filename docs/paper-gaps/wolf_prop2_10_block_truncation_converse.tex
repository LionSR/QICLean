\section{The printed equivalence}

Let $T:\MN{2}\to\MN{2}$ be a Hermiticity-preserving linear map. In the Pauli
basis $\{\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3\}$, where $\sigma_0=\Id$, define
its transfer matrix by
\begin{align}
    \widehat T_{ij}
        &=\frac{1}{2}\tr\!\left(\sigma_iT(\sigma_j)\right),
        \qquad 0\leq i,j\leq3.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_transfer_entries}
\end{align}
Write this matrix in block form as
\begin{align}
    \widehat T
        &=
        \begin{pmatrix}
            \widehat T_{00} & r^{\mathsf T}\\
            v & \Delta
        \end{pmatrix},
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_transfer_blocks}\\
    \Delta
        &=(\widehat T_{ij})_{i,j=1}^{3}.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_lower_block}
\end{align}
The vectors $r$ and $v$ form the off-diagonal blocks. This notation agrees
with the Bloch-ball affine representation $x\mapsto v+\Delta x$ in
Equation~(2.39) of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}.

Proposition~2.10 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} defines the Pauli-block
truncation by
\begin{align}
    \widehat T'
        &:=\widehat T_{00}\oplus\Delta,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_truncated_direct_sum}\\
    \widehat T'
        &=
        \begin{pmatrix}
            \widehat T_{00} & 0\\
            0 & \Delta
        \end{pmatrix}.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_truncated_matrix}
\end{align}
The proposition claims that $T'$ is positive, respectively completely
positive, if and only if $T$ has the corresponding property.\footnote{Local
source: \path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch02_representations.tex},
lines~984--990.}

\section{What the proof establishes}

Define the Pauli time-reversal map $\Theta:\MN{2}\to\MN{2}$ and its transfer
matrix $D$ by
\begin{align}
    \Theta(X)
        &=\tr(X)\Id-X,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_time_reversal}\\
    D
        &=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1).
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_time_reversal_matrix}
\end{align}
The transfer matrix of $\Theta$ is $D$. Consequently,
\begin{align}
    T'
        &=\frac{T+\Theta\circ T\circ\Theta}{2},
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_average_map}\\
    \widehat T'
        &=\frac{\widehat T+D\widehat T D}{2},
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_average_transfer}\\
    \frac{\widehat T+D\widehat T D}{2}
        &=\widehat T_{00}\oplus\Delta.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_average_truncation}
\end{align}
The proof of Proposition~2.10 in Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} uses this
average to establish
\begin{align}
    T\text{ positive}
        &\Longrightarrow T'\text{ positive},
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_positive_forward}\\
    T\text{ completely positive}
        &\Longrightarrow T'\text{ completely positive}.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_cp_forward}
\end{align}
Double time reversal preserves positivity and complete positivity in this
setting, and both cones are convex. This argument proves only the forward
implications
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_positive_forward}) and
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_cp_forward}); it proves
neither converse.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch02_representations.tex}, lines~992--998.}

The formalization follows this averaging argument. It defines Pauli time
reversal, its diagonal transfer coefficients, and the truncation through
\leanid{Wolf.pauliTimeReversal},
\leanid{Wolf.pauliTimeReversalDiagonal}, and
\leanid{Wolf.pauliBlockDiagonalTruncation}. The transfer-matrix identities
are \leanid{Wolf.pauliTransferEntry_timeReversal_comp} and
\leanid{Wolf.pauliTransferEntry_pauliBlockDiagonalTruncation}. The forward
preservation results are
\leanid{Wolf.pauliBlockDiagonalTruncation_isPositiveMap} and
\leanid{Wolf.pauliBlockDiagonalTruncation_isCPMap}.\footnote{All declarations
are in

\section{Counterexample to both converses}

Define
\begin{align}
    T(X)
        &=\tr(X)
          \begin{pmatrix}
              3/2 & 0\\
              0 & -1/2
          \end{pmatrix}.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_counterexample_map}
\end{align}
This map is Hermiticity preserving. It is not positive because
\begin{align}
    T(\Id/2)
        &=
          \begin{pmatrix}
              3/2 & 0\\
              0 & -1/2
          \end{pmatrix},
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_negative_output}\\
    T(\Id/2)
        &\not\geq0.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_not_positive}
\end{align}

Its transfer matrix has $\widehat T_{00}=1$ and $\Delta=0$; its only nonzero
off-diagonal data lie in the column $v$. Truncation therefore gives
\begin{align}
    \widehat T'
        &=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,0,0),
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_depolarizing_transfer}\\
    T'(X)
        &=\frac{\tr(X)}{2}\Id.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_depolarizing_map}
\end{align}
The map in
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_depolarizing_map}) is the
completely depolarizing channel and is completely positive. Thus $T'$ can be
completely positive while $T$ is not even positive. The converse fails for
both positivity and complete positivity.

\section{Conclusion}

The ``if and only if'' assertion in Proposition~2.10 of
Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} is false as printed. Its proof establishes only
the forward implications
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_positive_forward}) and
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_cp_forward}), which are the
statements formalized in Lean. The map
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop2_10_block_truncation_converse_counterexample_map}) shows
that no converse follows from Pauli-block truncation. Both printed
equivalences should be replaced by forward implications.
