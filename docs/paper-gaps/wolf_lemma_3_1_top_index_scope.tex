\section{Source statement}

Let $D',D$ be finite dimensions, and let
$\phi\in\mathbb C^{D'}\otimes\mathbb C^D$ be a normalized vector. Define its
reduced density matrix on the first tensor factor by
\begin{align}
    \rho
        &=\tr_2(\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}).
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_reduced_density}
\end{align}
For $1\leq n\leq D'$, let $\|\rho\|_{(n)}$ denote the Ky Fan $n$-norm, the
sum of the $n$ largest eigenvalues of $\rho$. Wolf's Lemma~3.1 states
\begin{align}
    \sup_{\substack{\psi\ \mathrm{normalized}\\
                    \operatorname{SchmidtRank}(\psi)\leq n}}
        \left|\braket{\phi}{\psi}\right|^2
        &=\|\rho\|_{(n)}.
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_source_identity}
\end{align}
The local source is
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch03_positive_not_completely.tex},
lines~119--128; see Lemma~3.1 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}.

Because $\rho$ acts on $\mathbb C^{D'}$, its Ky Fan $n$-norm is defined
throughout the range $1\leq n\leq D'$. In the application to $n$-positivity
in Wolf's Proposition~3.2, the Schmidt-rank index has the same range
\cite[Proposition~3.2]{Wolf2012Quantum}. In particular, the endpoint $n=D'$
belongs to the source statement.

\section{Originally formalized range}

The theorem
\leanid{Matrix.maximalSchmidtOverlap_eq_kyFanNorm} establishes
Eq.~\ref{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_source_identity} and strengthens
the supremum assertion to attainment through Lean's \texttt{IsGreatest}
predicate.\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{Matrix.maximalSchmidtOverlap_eq_kyFanNorm} in
    \doclink{QICLean/Channel/MaximalOverlap}.}
Its hypotheses originally covered
\begin{align}
    1\leq n
        &<D'.
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_original_formal_range}
\end{align}
Thus the formal theorem omitted only the endpoint $n=D'$.

\section{The endpoint}

At $n=D'$, every vector in
$\mathbb C^{D'}\otimes\mathbb C^D$ has Schmidt rank at most $D'$, so the
Schmidt-rank constraint is vacuous. The normalized choice $\psi=\phi$ gives
\begin{align}
    \left|\braket{\phi}{\phi}\right|^2
        &=1.
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_endpoint_attainment}
\end{align}
Cauchy--Schwarz gives the matching upper bound
\begin{align}
    \left|\braket{\phi}{\psi}\right|^2
        &\leq
        \braket{\phi}{\phi}\braket{\psi}{\psi}
        =1
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_cauchy_schwarz_bound}
\end{align}
for every normalized $\psi$. Since $\rho$ is a density matrix,
\begin{align}
    \|\rho\|_{(D')}
        &=\tr(\rho)
        =1.
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_top_ky_fan_trace}
\end{align}
Equations~\ref{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_endpoint_attainment}--%
\ref{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_top_ky_fan_trace} prove the source
identity at the endpoint.

The original restriction came from the underlying maximum principle for Ky
Fan norms.\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{Matrix.IsHermitian.kyFanNorm_eq_sup_trace}.}
That theorem characterizes the Ky Fan $k$-norm as a maximum over orthogonal
projections of rank exactly $k$ only when $k<D'$. At $k=D'$, the only
rank-$D'$ orthogonal projection is the identity, and the variational value
reduces to
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{Re}\tr(A).
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_top_projection_value}
\end{align}
The variational statement degenerates at the endpoint; it does not fail.

\section{Resolution}

The companion theorem
\leanid{Matrix.maximalSchmidtOverlap_eq_kyFanNorm_top} now proves the endpoint
$n=D'$. It uses the vacuous Schmidt-rank constraint, the maximizing vector
$\psi=\phi$, the upper bound in
Eq.~\ref{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_cauchy_schwarz_bound}, and the
trace identity in
Eq.~\ref{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_top_ky_fan_trace}.

Together, the declarations
\leanid{Matrix.maximalSchmidtOverlap_eq_kyFanNorm} and
\leanid{Matrix.maximalSchmidtOverlap_eq_kyFanNorm_top} cover
\begin{align}
    1\leq n
        &\leq D',
    \label{eq:wolf_lemma_3_1_top_index_scope_resolved_full_range}
\end{align}
which is the full range of Wolf's Lemma~3.1
\cite[Lemma~3.1]{Wolf2012Quantum}.

\section{Classification}

The former omission was a scope restriction in the formalization, not an
error in the source theorem. It is resolved by the endpoint companion theorem,
exactly as anticipated by the plan to treat the identity projection
separately.
