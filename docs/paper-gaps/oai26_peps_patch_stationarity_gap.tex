\section{Source argument}
The September 24, 2026 manuscript \emph{Polynomial PEPS approximation of
gapped square-grid ground states} minimizes the norm of an ordered product
of regional shifted powers. In \path{03-patches.tex}, lines 130--205,
descending trace calculations give commuting regional minimizing densities
$x_j$ and output marginals $\rho_j$. The eigenvalue conditions
\texttt{eq:patch-simplex-derivative}, \texttt{eq:patch-kkt}, and
\texttt{eq:patch-clipped-eigenvalues} imply $\rho_j\le x_j+bI$.
The source is fixed at commit
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a} of
\href{https://github.com/openai/math}{openai/math}.

\section{Proved operator consequences}
The results in \path{QICLean/Analysis/PatchRegulator.lean} prove the following
finite-dimensional implications. For commuting positive matrices $x,\rho$,
$b>0$, and $\rho\le x+bI$, the actual regulator
$T=b\rho(x+bI)^{-1}$ satisfies $0\le T\le bI$ and $T\le\rho$.
For every orthogonal projection $Q$,
\begin{align}
 \operatorname{tr}T\le b\operatorname{rank}Q+
   \operatorname{tr}((I-Q)\rho).
\end{align}
This estimate does not require $Q$ to commute with $T$ or $\rho$.
The source's exponential rank and constant-tail inequalities imply
$\operatorname{tr}T\le1/4$ at its stated starting radius. These are
auxiliary implications with their hypotheses explicitly stated; they do not
assert those hypotheses at every minimizer of the ordered patch objective.

\section{Required continuation}
The remaining work is to derive the commutation and derivative identities
for the actual ordered objective by descending trace calculations, with
the same normalized output marginals as in the source. Closed-simplex
first-order conditions can then imply the density order, and the regulator
estimate can be applied without an additional hypothesis on minimizers.
A separable scalar shifted-power objective is sufficient for the last-filter
calculation; it must not be identified with the full ordered objective
without this argument. Until the continuation is proved, the unconditional
active-minimizer trace bound and Proposition 4.1 remain unformalized.
