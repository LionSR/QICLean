\section{Original statement}

Yamagami's Lemma~6 considers the cyclic matrix
\begin{equation}
  S_{n,m,s}=(s-m)I+C^l+C^{l+1}+\cdots+C^{l+m-1},
  \qquad 1\le m\le n-l,\quad n\le s,
  \label{eq:yamagami-matrix}
\end{equation}
and states that every eigenvalue other than the row sum $s$ lies in the open
disk
\begin{equation}
  \left|\lambda-\frac{s}{2}\right|<\frac{s}{2}.
  \label{eq:yamagami-open-disk}
\end{equation}
This is the spectral condition of Corollary~5 in the same paper
\cite[pp.~523--524]{Yamagami1993Cyclic}.

\section{False stride-one endpoint}

Set $l=1$, $m=n-1$, and $s=n$, which is included in the printed range.  Then
\begin{equation}
  S_{n,n-1,n}=I+C+C^2+\cdots+C^{n-1}.
\end{equation}
For every nontrivial standard character of $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$, the
corresponding eigenvalue is zero.  Consequently
\begin{equation}
  \left|0-\frac{n}{2}\right|=\frac{n}{2},
\end{equation}
so the strict inequality in~\eqref{eq:yamagami-open-disk} is false at this
endpoint.  This is also the
place where the strict triangle-inequality step in the printed proof loses all
strictness.

\section{Correction used by QICLean}

The Choi-type application needs only stride one and $m\le n-2$.  In exactly
that range, with $n\ge3$ and $s\ge n$, the declaration
\begin{center}
  \leanid{Yamagami.norm\_symbol\_sub\_half\_lt\_half}
\end{center}
in \doclink{QICLean/Analysis/CyclicReciprocal}
proves~\eqref{eq:yamagami-open-disk} for every nonzero
standard character.  It follows Yamagami's two cases at $s=n$ and then uses
the source's translation argument from $n$ to $s$.

The omitted stride-one endpoint $m=n-1$ does satisfy~(2) when $s>n$: its
nontrivial-character eigenvalue is $s-n$, and
\begin{equation}
  \left|s-n-\frac{s}{2}\right|<\frac{s}{2}
\end{equation}
is equivalent to $0<n<s$.  Thus the corrected stride-one alternatives are
$m\le n-2$ with $s\ge n$, or the endpoint $m=n-1$ with $s>n$.

\section{Scope}

\textbf{Verdict: false source endpoint, resolved for the required stride-one
range.}  This correction concerns only Lemma~6's open-disk condition.  It
does not by itself prove Yamagami's cyclic reciprocal inequality: the Nowosad
critical-point step and the regular and singular projective-boundary arguments
remain separate dependencies.
