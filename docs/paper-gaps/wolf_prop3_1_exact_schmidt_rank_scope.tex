\section{The printed criterion}

Let $T:M_d(\C)\to M_{d'}(\C)$ be linear. Define its Choi matrix by
\begin{align}
    \tau_T
        &=(T\otimes\operatorname{id}_d)
          (\ketbra{\Omega_d}{\Omega_d}),
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_choi_definition}\\
    \tau_T
        &\in M_{d'}(\C)\otimes M_d(\C).
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_choi_space}
\end{align}
Proposition~3.1 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} assumes $k\leq d$ and includes
the condition
\begin{align}
    \bra{\psi}\tau_T\ket{\psi}
        &\geq0
        \qquad\text{whenever }\operatorname{SR}(\psi)=k,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_printed_choi_condition}
\end{align}
where $\operatorname{SR}(\psi)$ denotes the Schmidt rank. This is condition~(3)
in the printed proposition. The local source uses ``Schmidt-rank $n$'' in
both the statement and the final sentence of the proof.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch03_positive_not_completely.tex},
lines~89--115. Tracked by
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/QICLean/issues/24}{QICLean issue \#24}.}

\section{The dimension defect}

Every $\psi\in\C^{d'}\otimes\C^d$ satisfies
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{SR}(\psi)
        &\leq\min\{d',d\}.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_rank_dimension_bound}
\end{align}
If $d'<k\leq d$, no vector has Schmidt rank exactly $k$. The condition
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_printed_choi_condition}) is
then vacuous and cannot characterize $k$-positivity.

For example, set
\begin{align}
    d&=2,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_counterexample_input_dimension}\\
    d'&=1,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_counterexample_output_dimension}\\
    k&=2,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_counterexample_rank}\\
    T(X)&=-\tr(X).
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_counterexample_map}
\end{align}
There is no Schmidt-rank-two vector in $\C^1\otimes\C^2$, so the printed
quadratic-form condition holds. However, applying
$T\otimes\operatorname{id}_2$ to a nonzero product projector produces a
negative rank-one matrix. Thus $T$ is not $2$-positive.

The final step of the printed proof has the same defect. A vector of the form
\begin{align}
    \psi
        &=(\one\otimes X)^\dagger\widetilde\psi
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_compressed_vector}
\end{align}
satisfies
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{SR}(\psi)
        &\leq\operatorname{rank}(X),
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_compressed_rank_bound}
\end{align}
but equality need not hold. The compression argument directly proves a
bounded-rank criterion, not the printed exact-rank criterion.

\section{The corrected statement}

The dimension-generic Choi criterion is
\begin{align}
    T\text{ is $k$-positive}
        &\Longleftrightarrow
          \bra{\psi}\tau_T\ket{\psi}\geq0
          \quad\text{for every $\psi$ with }
          \operatorname{SR}(\psi)\leq k.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_corrected_choi_criterion}
\end{align}
This is the condition proved by the compression argument. Factoring the
coefficient matrix of $\psi$ through $\C^k$ gives
\begin{align}
    \psi
        &=R_X^\dagger\eta,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_coefficient_factorization}
\end{align}
and positivity of the compressed Choi matrix
\begin{align}
    R_X\tau_T R_X^\dagger
        &\geq0
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_positive_compression}
\end{align}
implies the quadratic-form inequality in
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_corrected_choi_criterion}).

For $d>0$, the formal theorem permits independent input and output dimensions,
including $k=0$ and $d'=0$.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{ChoiJamiolkowski.isNPositiveMap_iff_forall_hasSchmidtRankLE_choiMatrix_quadraticForm_nonneg_rectangular}
and
\leanid{ChoiJamiolkowski.isNPositiveMap_iff_forall_rankProjection_choiMatrix_sandwich_posSemidef_rectangular}
in \doclink{QICLean/Channel/Schwarz/ChoiCompression}.}
The projection formulation additionally assumes $k\leq d$, which is precisely
the condition required to choose a rank-$k$ projection on the input factor.

If $k\leq d'$ as well, the exact-rank condition follows from the bounded-rank
condition by a separate density argument: coefficient matrices of rank exactly
$k$ are dense among coefficient matrices of rank at most $k$, and the
quadratic form is continuous. This is not the proof printed in Proposition~3.1
of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}, and the formal criterion does not use it.

\section{The same defect in Proposition~3.3}

Let $\rho\in M_d(\C)\otimes M_{d'}(\C)$ be a density operator.
Proposition~3.3 of Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} states that $\rho$ has Schmidt
number at least $n+1$ if and only if there is a Hermitian operator $W$ such
that
\begin{align}
    \tr(W\rho)
        &<0,
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_printed_witness_detection}\\
    \bra{\psi}W\ket{\psi}
        &\geq0
        \qquad\text{whenever }\operatorname{SR}(\psi)=n.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_printed_witness_rank}
\end{align}
The source defines $S_n$ as the density operators of Schmidt number at most
$n$ and separates $\rho$ from $S_n$. Its converse identifies $S_n$ with the
closed convex hull of the same printed exact-rank set.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch03_positive_not_completely.tex},
lines~229--243. Tracked by
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/QICLean/issues/23}{QICLean issue \#23}.}

This exact-rank wording fails when $n>\min\{d,d'\}$. No vector then has
Schmidt rank exactly $n$, so
(\ref{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_printed_witness_rank}) is
vacuous. Taking
\begin{align}
    W
        &=-\one
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_vacuous_witness}
\end{align}
gives
\begin{align}
    \tr(W\rho)
        &=-1<0
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_vacuous_detection}
\end{align}
for every density operator, although no such state can have Schmidt number at
least $n+1$. The printed equivalence is therefore false whenever its test set
is empty.

The dimension-generic correction is
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{sn}(\rho)>n
        &\Longleftrightarrow
          \exists\,W=W^\dagger:\,
          \tr(W\rho)<0
          \text{ and }
          \bra{\psi}W\ket{\psi}\geq0
          \text{ whenever }\operatorname{SR}(\psi)\leq n.
    \label{eq:wolf_prop3_1_exact_schmidt_rank_scope_corrected_witness_criterion}
\end{align}
This is the separating-hyperplane criterion for $S_n$, because each pure
projector in a defining decomposition of $S_n$ has Schmidt rank at most $n$.
It is formalized by
\leanid{Matrix.not_hasSchmidtNumberLE_iff_exists_witness} in
\doclink{QICLean/Channel/EntanglementWitness}. It also supplies the witness
used in the rectangular positive-map characterization
\leanid{Matrix.hasSchmidtNumberLE_iff_forall_isNPositiveMap_tensorMapId_posSemidef}
in \doclink{QICLean/Channel/PositiveMapDetection}.

When $n\leq\min\{d,d'\}$, density and continuity again recover the exact-rank
test from the bounded-rank test. This additional argument is not the
separating-hyperplane proof printed in Proposition~3.3 of
Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}, and the formal theorem does not use it.

\section{Conclusion}

The exact-rank wording in Proposition~3.1 of
Ref.~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum} is false under the printed hypothesis $k\leq d$.
The same wording in Proposition~3.3 is false when
$n>\min\{d,d'\}$. Replacing exact Schmidt rank by Schmidt rank at most the
specified bound gives dimension-generic Choi and witness criteria and matches
the respective compression and convex-separation proofs. The formal
statements and the Blueprint use this corrected wording; they do not attribute
an additional argument to the source.
