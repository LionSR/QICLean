\section{Source statement}
For positive semidefinite $\rho,\sigma\in M_d(\mathbb C)$ Wolf
\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}%
\footnote{\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch08_distance_measures.tex},
lines~466--506, Equation~(8.38) and the footnote on lines~473--475.}
sets
\begin{align}
    S^{(\alpha)}(\rho|\sigma)
        &= \frac{\tr[\rho-\rho^\alpha\sigma^{1-\alpha}]}{1-\alpha},
    &
    S(\rho|\sigma) &= \tr[\rho(\log\rho-\log\sigma)],
    \label{eq:tnef_definitions}
\end{align}
and states $S^{(\alpha)}(\rho|\sigma)\le S(\rho|\sigma)$ for all $\alpha\le1$.
For exponents outside $[0,1]$ the footnote takes inverses on the support and
sets the quantity to $+\infty$ when the kernel of the inverted operator is
not contained in the kernel of the other operator.

\section{Counterexample}
Let $\alpha<0$, $\rho=\lvert0\rangle\langle0\rvert$ and $\sigma=\mathbb 1/2$ in
$M_2(\mathbb C)$. The power $\rho^\alpha$ inverts $\rho$, and
$\ker\rho\not\subseteq\ker\sigma=\{0\}$, so the footnote gives
$S^{(\alpha)}(\rho|\sigma)=+\infty$. On the other hand $\ker\sigma\subseteq\ker\rho$
and
\begin{align}
    S(\rho|\sigma) &= \tr\bigl[\rho\log\rho\bigr]-\tr\bigl[\rho\log(\mathbb 1/2)\bigr]
        = 0+\log2=\log2 .
    \label{eq:tnef_counterexample}
\end{align}
The printed inequality would assert $+\infty\le\log2$.

\section{Correction}
With the support-inverse convention alone, so that $\rho^\alpha$ is the
inverse power on the support of $\rho$ and vanishes on its kernel, the left
inequality holds for every $\alpha<1$ whenever $\ker\sigma\subseteq\ker\rho$:
each term of the spectral double sum satisfies
\begin{align}
    \lambda-\lambda^\alpha\mu^{1-\alpha}
        &\le(1-\alpha)\,\lambda(\log\lambda-\log\mu),
    \label{eq:tnef_scalar}
\end{align}
and the terms with $\lambda=0$ contribute $-0^\alpha\mu^{1-\alpha}=0\le0$.
In the counterexample this gives $S^{(\alpha)}(\rho|\sigma)=\tfrac{1}{1-\alpha}
(1-2^{\alpha-1})\le\log2$. The formal statement in
\doclink{QICLean/Analysis/RelativeEntropyRelations} uses this convention.

\section{Verdict}
False as printed for $\alpha<0$ under the footnote's infinite assignment;
resolved by using the support-inverse convention without that assignment.
The remaining scope restrictions of the theorem are recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/wolf_ch8_relative_entropy_relations.tex}.
