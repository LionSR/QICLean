\section{Source theorem and witness}

Wolf's Example~3.1 states that, for $d\ge3$ and $1\le n\le d-2$, the
Choi-type map
\begin{align}
    T_C(X)
     & =(d-n)D(X)-X+\sum_{k=1}^{n}D(U_{k0}XU_{k0}^{\dagger})
    \tag{3.20}
\end{align}
is positive and indecomposable\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}.
The local source is
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch03_positive_not_completely.tex}, lines
357--365. Wolf gives neither a proof nor a citation in that paragraph.

Ha proves the stronger atomicity assertion for this family
\cite[Theorem~2.1, pp.~593--595]{Ha1998AtomicPositiveMaps}. For
$0<\gamma<1$, the proof chooses all $3^d$-th roots of unity, forms vectors
$z_{r,i}$ that are 2-simple, and sets
\begin{align}
    A_r
     & =\frac{1}{3^d}\sum_{i=1}^{3^d}\ketbra{z_{r,i}}{z_{r,i}},
     &
    A_\gamma
     & =\frac1d\sum_{r=1}^{d}A_r.
    \label{eq:ha98_root_average}
\end{align}
Thus $A_\gamma$ belongs to the cone $V_2$ generated by projectors onto
2-simple vectors. Ha then derives the block entries of $A_\gamma$ from the
root power sum and the exponent-collision identity in equations (2.1)--(2.3).

Writing $A^T$ for Ha's block transpose, which is transposition in the second
tensor factor, the source next defines
\begin{align}
    u_i
     & =\frac{\gamma}{\sqrt d}e_{i+1}\otimes e_i
    +\frac{1}{\sqrt d\,\gamma}e_i\otimes e_{i+1}, \\
    v_i
     & =\sqrt{\frac{d-1}{d}}
    \bigl(e_{i+1}\otimes e_i+e_i\otimes e_{i+1}\bigr),
     &
    \alpha_i
     & =e_i\otimes e_i,
    \label{eq:ha98_uv_alpha}
\end{align}
with cyclic suffixes, together with the displayed vectors $\beta_{1j}$ and
$\beta_{ij}$ for non-neighbouring pairs. The calculation on p.~595 is
\begin{align}
    A_\gamma^T
     & =\sum_{i=1}^{d}
    \bigl(\ketbra{u_i}{u_i}+\ketbra{v_i}{v_i}
    +\ketbra{\alpha_i}{\alpha_i}\bigr)
    +\sum_{j=3}^{d-1}\ketbra{\beta_{1j}}{\beta_{1j}}
    +\sum_{i=2}^{d-2}\sum_{j=i+2}^{d}
    \ketbra{\beta_{ij}}{\beta_{ij}}.
    \label{eq:ha98_block_transpose}
\end{align}
It follows that the block transpose also belongs to $V_2$. Finally Ha
computes
\begin{align}
    \langle A_\gamma,T_C\rangle
     & =\gamma^2-1<0.
    \label{eq:ha98_negative_pairing}
\end{align}

\section{Formalized part}

Ha's source uses the one-based integers
$m_k=\frac32(3^{k-1}-1)$ and base vector $a_{i,1}$. The formalization shifts
all indices to zero-based notation, so its corresponding formula is
$m_k=\frac32(3^k-1)$ and its base vector is $a_{i,0}$. The root family, the
vectors $a_{i,k}$, $c_r$, $b_{r,i,j}$, and $z_{r,i}$, and the averages in
(\ref{eq:ha98_root_average}) are defined with this convention. The cyclic
coordinates use $\mathbb Z/d\mathbb Z$, so the shift convention agrees with
Wolf's equation (2.24) and Ha's rotation $S e_i=e_{i+1}$.

For fixed $r$ and $i$, every column of $z_{r,i}$ other than column $r$ is a
scalar multiple of $a_{i,0}$, while column $r$ is $c_r\circ a_{i,r}$.
Consequently the coefficient matrix has rank at most two. Averaging the
corresponding rank-one projectors proves
\begin{align}
    A_\gamma\in V_2,
    \qquad
    A_\gamma\ge0.
    \label{eq:ha98_formalized_slice}
\end{align}
The formalization also proves Ha's root-average entry formula (2.3), defines
the vectors $u_i$, $v_i$, $\alpha_i$, and $\beta_{i,j}$ with precisely the
two displayed beta ranges translated to zero-based indices, and proves
\begin{align}
    A_\gamma^{T_2}
     & =\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}
       \bigl(\ketbra{u_i}{u_i}+\ketbra{v_i}{v_i}
       +\ketbra{\alpha_i}{\alpha_i}\bigr)
       +\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal B_d}
       \ketbra{\beta_{i,j}}{\beta_{i,j}}
       \in V_2,
    \label{eq:ha98_formalized_block_transpose}
\end{align}
where $\mathcal B_d$ is the union of Ha's two displayed ranges. Here $T_2$
is the repository's right partial transpose. At the boundary $d=3$, the beta
range is empty, exactly as in the source. Thus both source-ordered
$V_2$ decompositions on pp.~594--595 are formalized.\footnote{Formal declarations:

\section{Factor swap, pairing, and remaining scope}

The bilinear duality used by Ha is fixed by Eom--Kye
\cite[equations (12)--(13), p.~137; Theorem~3.3 and the concluding shuffled
    pairing, pp.~138--139]{EomKye2000Duality}.
If $\sigma$ swaps the two tensor factors and
$J_d=\sum_{i,j}e_{ij}\otimes e_{ij}$, their orientation is
\begin{align}
    \langle A,\Phi\rangle
     & =\bigl\langle(\Phi\otimes\operatorname{id})(A^\sigma),J_d\bigr\rangle.
    \label{eq:ha98_eom_kye_orientation}
\end{align}
Thus the state tested by the ampliation is $\rho=A_\gamma^\sigma$, not
$A_\gamma$. Since Ha's block transpose is the second-factor partial
transpose,
\begin{align}
    \rho^{T_1}
     & =\bigl(A_\gamma^{T_2}\bigr)^\sigma.
    \label{eq:ha98_partial_transpose_orientation}
\end{align}
Equations~(\ref{eq:ha98_formalized_slice}) and
(\ref{eq:ha98_formalized_block_transpose}) supply the two source matrices
that must be transported through the factor swap. The formalization proves
that $\rho=A_\gamma^\sigma$ belongs to $V_2$ and that
$\rho^{T_1}=(A_\gamma^{T_2})^\sigma$ also belongs to $V_2$.

The maximally entangled vector in the formal development is normalized:
$\ket{\Omega}=d^{-1/2}\sum_i e_i\otimes e_i$. Hence Eom--Kye's matrix is
\begin{align}
    J_d
     & =d\ketbra{\Omega}{\Omega}.
    \label{eq:ha98_omega_normalization}
\end{align}
The positive factor $d$ is retained in the formalized identity. For the full
source range $d\ge3$, $1\le n\le d-2$, and $\gamma>0$, it proves
\begin{align}
    \langle A_\gamma,T_C\rangle
     & =d\,\bra{\Omega}
       (T_C\otimes\operatorname{id})(A_\gamma^\sigma)\ket{\Omega}
      =\gamma^2-1.
    \label{eq:ha98_formalized_negative_pairing}
\end{align}
Thus $0<\gamma<1$ gives the strict negativity in
(\ref{eq:ha98_negative_pairing}).\footnote{The factor swap, partial-transpose
    transport, pairing identity, cyclic-coordinate calculation, and normalized
    quadratic form are in
    \doclink{QICLean/Channel/ChoiTypeMap/HaNegativePairing}; the principal
    declarations are
    \leanid{Matrix.partialTransposeLeft\_tensorFactorSwap\_haAGamma\_hasSchmidtNumberLE\_two},
    \leanid{Matrix.eomKyePairing\_eq\_omegaVec\_quadraticForm\_factorSwap},
    \leanid{Matrix.eomKyePairing\_haAGamma\_choiTypeMap}, and
    \leanid{Matrix.choiTypeMapFin\_haAGamma\_omegaVec\_quadraticForm\_re\_neg}.}

This completes the source algebra of the witness, but it does not prove that
$T_C$ is positive. Positivity in the middle range $2\le n\le d-3$ remains
the content of \ghissue{19}. After that hypothesis is available, a final
theorem combining positivity with the negative witness to conclude
indecomposability still remains to be stated; this is tracked by
\ghissue{18}. No atomicity conclusion is asserted by the present
formalization.

\section{Classification}

\emph{Scope restriction.} The exact root-average construction, its entry
formula, both $V_2$ decompositions on pp.~594--595, their tensor-factor
transport, the Eom--Kye orientation, the normalization factor $d$, and the
value $\gamma^2-1$ are formalized for every $d\ge3$ and
$1\le n\le d-2$. The middle-range positivity theorem and the final
indecomposability theorem remain open. No atomicity conclusion and no
alternative Choi-matrix or partial-transpose ansatz is asserted.
