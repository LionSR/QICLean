# Permutation symmetry of component marginals

The partial trace of the rank-one density of a simultaneously fixed vector
commutes with the permutation on the retained register. This follows from
the literal outer-product identity and partial-trace covariance; no
normalization or independent-copy assumption is needed.

For the actual excitation component `(R_B ⊗ I)v`, the fixed-vector equation
is derived from the symmetry of `v` and the condition that the permutation
stabilizes `B`. Its reduced density is constructed by taking the actual
partial trace. Component invariance or marginal invariance is not supplied
as an additional hypothesis.

These two identities give the partial-trace step following equation
`comparator:merge-moments` in the September 24, 2026 manuscript
*A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap*, Section 7,
lines 540–549. They are auxiliary statements, rather than the merge-moment
estimate. Selection and regrouping of the good-copy registers, their use
with the physical prevector, and the subsequent representation-theoretic
moment bound remain separate obligations. The statements include zero
copies, empty registers and zero vectors.
