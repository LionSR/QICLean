# Quantitative Schur-label moment bounds

Let ρ be a permutation-invariant density on k copies of a q-dimensional space. Write F for its Schur-label observable and L for the negative logarithm of ρ, extended by zero on the kernel. For an arbitrary real center s, suppose

\[
\log\operatorname{ReTr}(\rho e^{t(L-sI)})\le C_0 k\mathcal B t^2,
\qquad |t|\le c_0/\sqrt{\mathcal B},
\]

where C₀≥0, c₀>0 and ℬ≥1. With c=min(c₀,1)/2, both signs satisfy

\[
\log\operatorname{ReTr}(\rho e^{t(F-sI)})
\le 2C_0k\mathcal B t^2+\tfrac12q^2\log(k+1),
\qquad |t|\le c/\sqrt{\mathcal B}.
\]

The positive sign has the smaller quadratic coefficient C₀. The negative sign follows from the signed Schur-label comparison and the polynomial Schur remainder. All logarithms of exponential trace moments are justified by strict positivity, derived from positivity and trace one of ρ. Singular states and zero copies are included.
