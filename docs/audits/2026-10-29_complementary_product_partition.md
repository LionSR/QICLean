# Partitioning a commuting complementary product

A finite complementary product of pairwise commuting bounded operators
splits into the products selected by any predicate and its complement.
The identity includes empty products. It requires neither projections,
finite dimension nor completeness of the real or complex inner-product
space. The proof uses the existing complementary-product recursion and
commutation theorem, followed by associativity of composition.

This is the elementary regrouping needed for ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2,
`def:FF-tH` and `thm:FF-tH`, lines 1467–1487 and 1543–1550. The subsequent
physical application will split each actual parity into inside and
outside bonds, without accessing another module's private declarations.
The regional gap and corrected rank theorem are not asserted here.
