# Ritz extraction from a close candidate

For a Hermitian Hamiltonian with ground energy mu and gap gamma > 0,
a candidate W that is rho-close to the full ground space, 0 ≤ rho < 1,
determines an actual ground-rank compressed Ritz space R inside W.
Its two projection errors are at most

    (||H|| − mu) rho / ((1 − rho) gamma).

The trial space is the literal orthogonal image of the full ground space
inside W; its dimension and energy bound are proved. No trial-space energy
hypothesis, chosen compression, compressed eigenbasis, unique ground state,
or output accuracy premise is supplied. The selection of R depends on the
original H, W and the actual ground-space dimension.

This completes the variational implication needed after a physical refinement.
The physical construction of W, its MPS bounds, the printed refinement count
and the probability and running-time assertions remain separate obligations.
