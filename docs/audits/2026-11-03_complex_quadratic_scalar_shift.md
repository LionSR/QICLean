# Complex matrix specialization of quadratic scalar shifts

The existing four real-or-complex operator and matrix identities are
unchanged. The new concrete complex matrix identity states the subtraction
formula with the ordinary complex embedding of the real coefficient and
the complex real part. It follows from the existing general matrix theorem
by the standard algebra-map and real-part identities. There is no second
quadratic-form calculation and no additional hypothesis.

Three actual regional-energy consumers use this form: the original native
compression estimate, the central soft-parent estimate, and the new raw
word-candidate compression. This removes their repeated scalar-shift
expansions and repeated coercion normalization. The generic formulas remain
available and are used by this specialization. No compatibility alias or
addition specialization without a consumer is introduced.
