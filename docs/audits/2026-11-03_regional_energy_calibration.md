# Regional energy through a nearby target and a candidate

Let C be positive with norm at most A. If its energy on the original ground space G is at most (2+rho) times the squared norm, with 0<=rho<=1/16, then a target T which is tau-close to G in the directed convention G close to T has energy at most 9/4 when 0<=tau<=1/[1024(A+1)]. If a candidate U is delta-close to T, with 0<=delta<=1/[100(A+1)], the actual image P_U T has energy at most three. The proof uses the existing weighted positive-form inequalities at weights 16 and 8. No comparison vector or trial-energy bound is supplied as a hypothesis.

This is an auxiliary for ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, the proof of Theorem t:alg1 at line 1807. It does not establish the physical norm, residual or closeness budgets, identify independently chosen operator families, or assert a running time. The projected space need not be assumed nonzero for the energy inequality; nonzero dimension and the actual compressed minimum belong to its physical application.
