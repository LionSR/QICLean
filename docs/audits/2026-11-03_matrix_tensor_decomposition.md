# Whole-matrix identities from canonical tensor decompositions

Two supporting lemmas recover the literal whole matrix of a supplied tensor
operator and pull its four invariant-subspace bounds back by the same
canonical unitary. The exterior coefficients are the matrices of the
supplied exterior maps. Neither lemma selects another filter or assumes a
new decomposition. Finite coordinate sets and the summation set may be empty.
