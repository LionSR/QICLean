# Estimated soft centers and a common commuting family

Two generic matrix results construct literal soft replacements for every
estimated center in the source's ten-unit interval. They derive corrected
positivity, upper order, commutant preservation and high-projection floors.
No projection inequality, successful approximation, spectral gap or reverse
residual is supplied. The common family is formed directly from the original
commuting regional matrices; a previously softened block is not treated as
though it were still a nearest-neighbour sum.

For a selected set S the positive correction is exactly 10 times its actual
cardinality. The proof sums the individually corrected positive terms. A
selected high-projection floor lies below its actual corrected term and then
below this actual sum. Every resulting term commutes with every original
regional matrix. This proves the common-interior structure needed for
simultaneous replacements, independently of any physical boundary theorem.
Empty families, empty selected sets and zero-dimensional matrix algebras
are allowed. Scale and order remain independent and explicit.

These are auxiliaries to ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, `def:soft-trunc` and
`t:aisefficient`, lines 1178–1185 and 1373–1392. They handle the full
estimated-center range for the generic order statements. They do not establish
the physical estimated-center residual, the common five-region boundary and
ground-energy bounds, the printed spectral enlargement or the full GEN
construction. The physical exact-center scope note remains open until those
physical substitutions are proved.
