# Energy on the actual projected ground-space trial

Let U be δ-close to a zero-energy target G, where 0≤δ<1, and let H be
symmetric, vanish on G, and have operator norm at most A. The public
statement identifies the actual image R=P_U G. It proves containment in U,
its dimension equal to dim G, and the uniform bound

    Re⟨Hx,x⟩ ≤ Aδ/(1−δ) ‖x‖²       for every x in R.

The dimension follows from injectivity of the restricted projection.
Pythagoras bounds the displacement of x=P_Ug from g, and symmetry removes
the cross terms involving Hg=0. The resulting inequality holds on the whole projected span.

This is an auxiliary variational comparison for the final r-dimensional
compressed spectral extraction in ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2,
Algorithm t:alg1, lines 1634–1640. For a physical Hamiltonian it is applied
after subtracting its ground energy. The extracted Ritz space and its
ground-space closeness remain subsequent steps.
