# A logarithmic power of the actual retained-factor family

The energy-estimation candidate in ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, Figure `fig:algo1`, line 1631, is a separate untrimmed operator-word image of the two child spaces. It is not the retained collectively trimmed output. The new bound reuses the existing ordered-word construction, its tensor decomposition and its dimension estimate.

For a fixed spectral filter with squared complementary contraction `0 <= Delta <= 1/4`, an initial viability error at most `1/2`, and requested accuracy `eta > 0`, choose the natural power `ceil(4 log(4/eta)) + 1`. The actual ordered-word image is `eta`-viable for the same target and has dimension at most the alphabet cardinality raised to that power, times the initial dimension. The scalar proof bounds `Delta^p` by `(3/4)^p <= exp(-p/4) <= eta/4`, while the initial squared norm denominator is at least `1/4`.

This is an explicit sufficient power. It does not identify the printed `4 ceil(log(1/gamma))` count, construct the generated soft Hamiltonian, compare its actual minimum with the original regional minimum, or close the recursive energy invariant. Those statements require the same physical operator and its scalar shifts to be tracked explicitly.
