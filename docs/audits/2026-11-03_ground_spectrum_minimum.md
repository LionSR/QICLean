# Spectral characterization of the ground energy

A Hermitian matrix on a nonzero finite-dimensional space has least eigenvalue
μ whenever μ belongs to its real spectrum and every real spectral value is
at least μ. The proof compares μ with an attained least eigenvalue. It assumes
no spectral separation and no restriction on the ground-space dimension.

construction. It selects neither an improving family nor a trial vector.
