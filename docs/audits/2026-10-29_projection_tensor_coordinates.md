# Isometric coordinates for a projection in two retained factors

`IsStarProjection.exists_tensor_range_isometries` constructs coordinates for
three orthogonal projections P,Q,R with (Q⊗R)P=P. It chooses actual range
isometries U,V,W, defines the coefficient matrix C=(V⊗W)ᴴU, and proves

    CᴴC=I,       U=(V⊗W)C,       rank(P)≤rank(Q)rank(R).

The chosen coordinate dimensions are exactly the ranks of the projections.
There is no nonzero-rank or nonempty-coordinate hypothesis. The statement
uses the existing matrix isometry predicate and range-coordinate theorem;
no parallel definition of isometry or projection is introduced.

The public compression identity
`Matrix.IsIsometry.conjTranspose_mul_of_range_eq` also works for fixed
coordinate maps: from UᴴU=I and AAᴴU=U it proves that AᴴU is an isometry
and U=A(AᴴU). This permits one simultaneous choice of range coordinates
at all hierarchy nodes. The existential theorem reuses this identity.

## The construction

Choose U,V,W by `IsStarProjection.exists_matrix_range_isometry`, so that
UUᴴ=P and similarly for Q,R, with orthonormal columns. Put A=V⊗W. The
Kronecker identities give AAᴴ=Q⊗R, while PU=U follows from UᴴU=I.
The parent containment therefore gives AAᴴU=U. For the displayed matrix
C=AᴴU, matrix associativity gives AC=U and CᴴC=I.

The rank identity for a matrix times its adjoint and the full column rank
of an isometry identify the three coordinate dimensions. The existing
dimension inequality for an isometry, applied to C, bounds the parent
dimension by the product of the two child dimensions. These identities
also apply when some dimensions vanish; no separate positive-rank premise
is used.

## Relation to the physical hierarchy

This is an auxiliary coordinate theorem for the expansion of a retained
parent basis in tensor products of retained child bases in Hastings,
*Solving gapped Hamiltonians locally*,
[arXiv:cond-mat/0508554v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0508554v3),
Section III, `proj3new.tex`, lines 630–685. The source explicitly leaves
bounds on the number of intermediate retained states open at lines 104–109.
The product dimension inequality is not a bound polynomial in physical
chain length.

The theorem is independent of a Hamiltonian. Applying it to the physical
hierarchy still requires extraction of each actual local projection from
its supported full-system operator, identification of the actual disjoint
child coordinates, and recursive contraction of the resulting tensors.
None of these physical conclusions is supplied as a hypothesis or claimed
as a consequence of the final global rank bound alone.
