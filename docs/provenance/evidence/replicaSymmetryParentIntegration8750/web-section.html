13.28 Copy symmetry and physical spectral cutoffs

Let \(\mathcal H=\mathbb C^A\) be finite-dimensional, and let \(U_\sigma \) be the operator permuting its \(k\ge 0\) copies by \(\sigma \in S_k\). Write \(H_\Sigma \) and \(N\) for the physical replica operators of Definition 13.24.1. The following commutation identities give the compatibility with symmetry and auxiliary sectors used in [ Ope26b , Section 7 ] . They require no normalization of \(\Omega \).

Theorem 13.28.1 Copy symmetry of a replica sum
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 13.24.1

Lean declarations

  • Matrix.commute_replicaHamiltonian_permOp

For every operator \(H\) on \(\mathcal H\) and every \(\sigma \in S_k\), \(H_\Sigma U_\sigma =U_\sigma H_\Sigma \).

Proof ▶

The \((x,y)\) matrix entry of \(H_\Sigma \) is \(\sum _i\prod _j K_{i,j}(x_j,y_j)\), where \(K_{i,i}=H\) and \(K_{i,j}=I\) for \(j\ne i\). Replacing \(x_j,y_j\) by \(x_{\sigma ^{-1}j},y_{\sigma ^{-1}j}\) and reindexing both \(i\) and \(j\) by \(\sigma ^{-1}\) leaves the sum unchanged. Thus \(H_\Sigma (\sigma x,\sigma y)=H_\Sigma (x,y)\), which is the stated commutation identity.

Theorem 13.28.2 Copy symmetry of a tensor power
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Matrix.commute_finKronecker_const_permOp

For every operator \(H\) on \(\mathcal H\) and every \(\sigma \in S_k\), \(H^{\otimes k}U_\sigma =U_\sigma H^{\otimes k}\). In particular, the independent-copy density \(\rho ^{\otimes k}\) is permutation invariant.

Proof ▶

The matrix entry is \(\prod _j H(x_j,y_j)\). Reindexing this product by \(\sigma ^{-1}\) proves invariance of the entries under simultaneous permutation of their two arguments, and hence commutation.

Theorem 13.28.3 Copy symmetry of functions of the excitation count
✓

For every real function \(f\) on the spectrum of \(N\) and every \(\sigma \in S_k\), \(f(N)U_\sigma =U_\sigma f(N)\). In particular, the closed spectral cutoff \(P_t=\mathbf1_{(-\infty ,t]}(N)\) commutes with every physical copy permutation.

Proof ▶

The operator \(N\) is the replica sum for \(I-|\Omega \rangle \langle \Omega |\), so it commutes with \(U_\sigma \). Real functional calculus preserves this commutation. Every function is continuous on the finite spectrum; no global continuity of the threshold indicator is needed.

Theorem 13.28.4 Physical functions and auxiliary operators

For any finite-dimensional auxiliary space \(\mathcal K\) and any operator \(M\) on it, \(f(N)\otimes I_{\mathcal K}\) commutes with \(U_\sigma \otimes M\).

Proof ▶

The two products are \((f(N)U_\sigma )\otimes M\) and \((U_\sigma f(N))\otimes M\), which agree.

Theorem 13.28.5 Preservation of fixed symmetry and auxiliary sectors
✓
#

  • Matrix.cfc_replicaDefectCount_kronecker_mulVec_preserves_fixed

If \((U_\sigma \otimes M)v=v\), then \((U_\sigma \otimes M)(f(N)\otimes I_{\mathcal K})v =(f(N)\otimes I_{\mathcal K})v\).

Proof ▶

Commute the two operators and use \((U_\sigma \otimes M)v=v\). Taking \(M\) to be the simultaneous copy action on the auxiliary systems proves preservation of the symmetric space. Taking \(\sigma \) to be the identity and \(M\) to be either auxiliary Schur projector proves preservation of its fixed sector. Both applications include \(f(N)=P_t\).

The concentration estimate used to select an entropy window, the defect-mass bound, the decomposition into ground factors, and the inverse-metric bounds are separate assertions.
