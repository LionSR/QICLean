\section{Quantitative bounds for the centered label moments}
\label{sec:schur-label-moment-bounds}

The following conditional estimate gives the scalar step in
\cite[Section~7, lines~227--238]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. Its hypothesis concerns
the centered surprisal on all copies. Deriving that hypothesis from the
one-copy marginal estimate by independence is a separate assertion.

\begin{theorem}[Quadratic label moments with an explicit logarithmic loss]
\label{thm:schur-label-moment-bounds}
\lean{TensorPower.log_centered_labelEntropy_moments_le,
TensorPower.log_centered_labelEntropy_moments_uniform}
\uses{thm:schur-label-copy-centered}
\leanok
Let $\rho$ be a permutation-invariant density matrix on $Q^{\otimes k}$,
where $\dim Q=q$. Write $F$ for its Schur-label observable and $L=-\log\rho$
on the support of $\rho$, with zero value on the kernel. For a real center
$s$, put
\[
 M_A(t;s)=\operatorname{ReTr}\bigl(\rho e^{t(A-sI)}\bigr).
\]
Suppose $C_0\ge0$, $c_0>0$, and $\mathcal B\ge1$, and assume the actual
centered surprisal estimate
\[
 \log M_L(t;s)\le C_0k\mathcal B t^2
 \qquad\left(|t|\le\frac{c_0}{\sqrt{\mathcal B}}\right).
\]
Then $c=\min(c_0,1)/2$ is positive. For $0\le u\le c/\sqrt{\mathcal B}$,
\begin{align*}
 \log M_F(u;s)&\le C_0k\mathcal B u^2,\\
 \log M_F(-u;s)&\le 2C_0k\mathcal B u^2
                  +\frac{q^2}{2}\log(k+1).
\end{align*}
In particular, throughout the symmetric interval
$|t|\le c/\sqrt{\mathcal B}$,
\[
 \log M_F(t;s)\le 2C_0k\mathcal B t^2
                      +\frac{q^2}{2}\log(k+1).
\]
The quadratic coefficient and the interval constant are independent of $q$.
The center is arbitrary; the specialization $s=kS(Q)$ requires the actual
independent-copy surprisal estimate at that center.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\leanok
Every Hermitian exponential is positive definite. Its trace pairing with
$\rho\ge0$, $\operatorname{Tr}\rho=1$, is therefore strictly positive,
so logarithmic monotonicity applies to the preceding moment comparisons.
Since $\sqrt{\mathcal B}\ge1$, the chosen range gives both $2u\le1$ and
$2u\le c_0/\sqrt{\mathcal B}$. The positive comparison immediately gives
the first bound. For the negative comparison,
\begin{align*}
 \log M_F(-u;s)
 &\le \tfrac12\log M_L(-2u;s)+\tfrac12 q^2\log(k+1)\\
 &\le 2C_0k\mathcal B u^2+\tfrac12 q^2\log(k+1).
\end{align*}
Finally, $C_0k\mathcal B t^2$ and $\log(k+1)$ are nonnegative, so the
larger bound also covers the positive sign. All zero-copy and singular-state
cases remain included.
\end{proof}
