13.20 Mass selection among Schur sectors

Let \(\Omega \) be a finite orthonormal basis of cardinality \(q\), and let \(\pi ^\lambda \) denote the central isotypic projectors for the copy-permutation representation on \((\mathbb C^\Omega )^{\otimes k}\), where \(k\ge 0\). Their sum is the identity, and the number of nonzero projectors is at most \((k+1)^{q^2}\). The following estimates give the generic mass selection step in [ OpenAI2026AreaLaw , Section 7 ] . They impose no entropy window on the irreducible dimension \(d_\lambda \). The asymptotic relation \(\log d_{\lambda _k}=kS'+o(k)\) requires concentration and a further selection within the corresponding window. Positive occurrence on the initial uniform pair, matching of its two labels, and its energy condition are separate assertions.

For every positive semidefinite \(\rho \) on \((\mathbb C^\Omega )^{\otimes k}\) with \(\operatorname {tr}\rho =1\), there is an irreducible label \(\lambda \) such that \(\operatorname {tr}(\rho \pi ^\lambda )\ge (k+1)^{-q^2}\).

Proof ▶

Put \(L=\{ \lambda :\pi ^\lambda \ne 0\} \) and \(m_\lambda =\operatorname {tr}(\rho \pi ^\lambda )\). Positivity gives \(m_\lambda \ge 0\), and completeness gives \(\sum _{\lambda \in L}m_\lambda =1\), so \(L\) is nonempty. Since \(|L|\le (k+1)^{q^2}\), at least one summand is at least \((k+1)^{-q^2}\).

Let \(S\) be a finite orthonormal basis and let \(v\) be a unit vector in \(\mathbb C^S\otimes (\mathbb C^\Omega )^{\otimes k}\). There is an irreducible label \(\lambda \) such that \(\| (I_S\otimes \pi ^\lambda )v\| ^2\ge (k+1)^{-q^2}\). In particular, \((I_S\otimes \pi ^\lambda )v\ne 0\).

Proof ▶

The reduced state \(\rho =\operatorname {tr}_S|v\rangle \langle v|\) is positive semidefinite and has trace one. Apply Theorem 13.20.1 to \(\rho \). For \(Q=I_S\otimes \pi ^\lambda \), the trace-pairing identity and the Hermitian idempotence \(Q^*=Q=Q^2\) give

\begin{align} \operatorname {tr}(\rho \pi ^\lambda ) & =\langle v,Qv\rangle =\langle Qv,Qv\rangle =\| Qv\| ^2. \label{eq:schur_sector_marginal_norm} \end{align}

The lower bound is strictly positive, so \(Qv\ne 0\).
