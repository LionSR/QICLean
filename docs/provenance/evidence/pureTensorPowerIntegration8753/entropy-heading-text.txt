Chapter 13

Quantum Entropy

This chapter develops trace distance, von Neumann entropy, and their basic properties for finite-
dimensional quantum systems.


13.1      Trace norm
Definition 13.1.1 (Schatten one-norm). Lean ✓ Stmt For a matrix 𝐴 ∈ 𝑀𝐷 (ℂ), the singular

                                               𝑖 ∶ 𝑠𝑖 (𝐴)≠0

Definition 13.1.2 (Trace norm).      Lean    ✓ Stmt The trace norm of 𝐴 ∈ 𝑀𝐷 (ℂ) is its Schatten
one-norm:

                                            ‖𝐴‖tr = ‖𝐴‖1 .                                 (13.2)

   Wolf records the equivalent formula ‖𝐴‖1 = tr|𝐴| [Wol12, Chapter 8, Section 8.1]; this is
Theorem 13.1.5.
Theorem 13.1.3 (Finite singular-value expansions). Lean Lean Lean ✓ Stmt The Schatten
one-norm and trace norm are the sums over the finite support of the singular-value sequence.
Equivalently, the trace norm is the sum over the indices below the rank of the represented linear
map.
Proof. ✓ Proof The singular-value sequence satisfies 𝑠𝑖 (𝐴) = 0 for all 𝑖 ≥ rank(𝐴), so summing
over the support and summing over {0, … , rank(𝐴) − 1} give the same value.
