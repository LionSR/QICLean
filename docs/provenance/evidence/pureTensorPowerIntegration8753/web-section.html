13.20 Reduced densities and masses of repeated pure states

Let \(\mathcal H_S=\mathbb C^S\) and \(\mathcal H_R=\mathbb C^\Omega \) be finite-dimensional, and let \(\psi \in \mathcal H_S\otimes \mathcal H_R\). Write \(\rho =\operatorname {tr}_S|\psi \rangle \langle \psi |\) for its actual reduced density, without imposing normalization. For \(k\ge 0\), regroup the repeated vector into the two systems. Its coordinates, indexed by \(x\in S^k\) and \(y\in \Omega ^k\), are

\begin{align} v_k(x,y)=\prod _{i=0}^{k-1}\psi (x_i,y_i). \end{align}

These are the pure-state identities used in the Schur-sector argument of [ Ope26b , Section 7 ] .

  • Matrix.partialTraceLeft_vecMulVec_prod

For every \(\psi \) and every \(k\ge 0\),

\begin{align} \operatorname {tr}_{S^k}|v_k\rangle \langle v_k|=\rho ^{\otimes k}. \end{align}
Proof ▶

For \(y,z\in \Omega ^k\), the matrix entry on the left is

\begin{align} \sum _{x\in S^k}\prod _i\psi (x_i,y_i)\overline{\psi (x_i,z_i)} =\prod _i\sum _{s\in S}\psi (s,y_i)\overline{\psi (s,z_i)} =\prod _i\rho (y_i,z_i). \end{align}

The middle equality is the distributive law for a finite product of sums.

  • Matrix.norm_sq_one_kronecker_mulVec_prod

For every orthogonal projector \(P\) on \(\mathcal H_R^{\otimes k}\),

\begin{align} \| (I_{S^k}\otimes P)v_k\| ^2 =\operatorname {Re}\operatorname {tr}(\rho ^{\otimes k}P). \end{align}

In particular, taking \(P\) to be an actual central Schur projector gives the squared norm in that sector.

Proof ▶

The trace-pairing property of the partial trace and the preceding identity give

\begin{align} \operatorname {tr}(\rho ^{\otimes k}P) =\langle v_k,(I_{S^k}\otimes P)v_k\rangle =\langle (I_{S^k}\otimes P)v_k,(I_{S^k}\otimes P)v_k\rangle . \end{align}

The second equality uses \(P=P^\dagger =P^2\). Taking real parts proves the formula.

Both identities include zero copies and empty one-copy factors; no invertibility of \(\rho \) is required. Concentration, the entropy window and selection of a common sequence of labels are separate assertions.
