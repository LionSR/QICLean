\subsection{Physical label moments of an excitation component}
\label{sec:replica_physical_label_moment}

Let $Q,T,K$ be finite coordinate sets, let $\Omega\in\mathbb C^Q\otimes\mathbb C^T$
be unit, and let $u\in(\mathbb C^Q\otimes\mathbb C^T)^{\otimes k}\otimes\mathbb C^K$.
For $B\subseteq\{0,\ldots,k-1\}$ put $G=B^c$, $m=|G|$, and
$w=(R_B\otimes I_K)u$. Choose a finite-set enumeration of $G$. Regroup $w$ as
$\widetilde w$ on
\[
 (Q^m\times K)\times(T^m\times(Q\times T)^B),
\]
using that same enumeration in both good-copy registers and retaining the
original bad-copy coordinates. Write
$\rho_Q=\operatorname{Tr}_T|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$ and let $F_Q^{(m)}$
be the logarithmic label-dimension observable for the actual permutations of
the $m$ good $Q$ copies. Its action on the whole regrouped space is
\[
 \widehat F_Q=(F_Q^{(m)}\otimes I_K)\otimes I_{T^m\times(Q\times T)^B}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact physical exponential moment]
\label{thm:replica_physical_label_moment}
\lean{Matrix.trace_exp_labelEntropy_replicaExcitationComponent_eq}
\leanok
For every $a\in\mathbb R$,
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(|\widetilde w\rangle\langle\widetilde w|
                       e^{a\widehat F_Q}\bigr)
 =\|w\|^2\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\rho_Q^{\otimes m}e^{aF_Q^{(m)}}\bigr).
 \label{eq:replica_physical_label_moment}
\end{equation}
No normalization or nonvanishing of $u$ or $w$ is required; zero copies are included.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\leanok
\uses{thm:replica_regional_product_density}
Tracing the complementary physical coordinates gives exactly
$\rho_Q^{\otimes m}\otimes\rho_K(w)$, where
$\rho_K(w)=\operatorname{Tr}_{(Q\times T)^k}|w\rangle\langle w|$ is the
literal exterior marginal of the same component. The exponential of the
retained-factor action is the exponential on $Q^m$ tensored with the identities
on every other factor. The partial-trace pairing and the trace of a tensor
product thus factor the left side into the iid regional moment and
$\operatorname{Tr}\rho_K(w)=\|w\|^2$. This proves the identity without division
by the component mass.
\end{proof}

This is the exact physical iid evaluation used in
\cite[Section~7, lines 556--560]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, following the regional
product argument at lines 520--549. Taking either sign of $a$ handles either
physical exponential factor. For the three-region partition $Q\otimes Y\otimes V$,
one uses the complements $Y\otimes V$, $Y\otimes Q$ and $Q\otimes V$ for the
respective retained regions $Q,V,Y$. The signed centred label-moment rate at
lines 227--237 and the five-factor comparison remain subsequent estimates.
