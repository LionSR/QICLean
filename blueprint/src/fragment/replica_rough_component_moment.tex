\section{A rough exponential estimate for an excitation component}
\label{sec:replica_rough_component_moment}

The following auxiliary estimate gives the spectral and moment steps in
\cite[Section~7, lines~513--555]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. The inverse metric and
the scalar factor from the good auxiliary labels remain separate arguments.

\begin{theorem}[Rough exponential estimate with the component mass]
\label{thm:replica_rough_component_moment}
\lean{Matrix.replicaGoodConfigurationMarginal_exp_mergeDeficits_sub_physicalMergeDeficit_le}
\uses{thm:replica_good_configuration_J_support,
thm:replica_good_configuration_sum_merge_moment,thm:spectral-exponential-drop-trace}
\leanok
Let $Q,Y,V,C,R$ be finite coordinate sets, let $\Omega$ be a unit vector
on $Q\otimes Y\otimes V$, and let $u$ be fixed by simultaneous permutation
of its physical, $C$ and $R$ copy strings. For a set $B$ of bad copies put
$m=|B^c|$ and $w=(R_B\otimes I)u$. Let $\Gamma$ be its actual density
retaining all five factors on the good copies and tracing only the bad
copies, with the same chosen enumeration in every factor. On this space put
\begin{align*}
 G_{\mathrm{phys}}&=F_Q+F_V-F_Y,\\
 D_C&=F_Q+F_C-F_{QC},\qquad D_R=F_V+F_R-F_{VR}.
\end{align*}
For $a\ge0$ with $2a\le1$,
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{ReTr}\bigl(\Gamma e^{a(D_C+D_R-G_{\mathrm{phys}})}\bigr)
 &\le \frac{(m+1)^{(|Q|\,|C|)^2}+(m+1)^{(|V|\,|R|)^2}}2\,\|w\|^2.
\end{align*}
Neither normalization nor nonvanishing of $w$ is required. The statement
includes zero copies and an empty set of good copies.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\leanok
The actual density is a partial trace of the positive rank-one matrix
of the rearranged component, and hence is positive semidefinite. Its
physical symmetric support gives
$\mathbf1_{[0,\infty)}(G_{\mathrm{phys}})\Gamma=\Gamma$.
The physical deficit commutes with both actual merge deficits, and all
three matrices are Hermitian. The supported exponential trace inequality
therefore gives
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{ReTr}\bigl(\Gamma e^{a(D_C+D_R-G_{\mathrm{phys}})}\bigr)
 &\le \operatorname{ReTr}\bigl(\Gamma e^{a(D_C+D_R)}\bigr).
\end{align*}
Apply the actual sum-merge moment bound to the same component $w$.
No positivity of $G_{\mathrm{phys}}$ outside the retained spectral subspace
is needed.
\end{proof}
