\section{Permutation symmetry of component marginals}
\label{sec:replica_marginal_symmetry}

The merge-moment argument in \cite[Section~7]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} uses
permutation invariance of an auxiliary marginal obtained by tracing other
registers. The following partial-trace identities do not require a product
state or normalization.

\begin{theorem}[Marginal symmetry of a fixed vector]
\label{thm:fixed_vector_marginal_symmetry}
\lean{PermutationRepresentation.commute_partialTraceLeft_vecMulVec_of_fixed_kronecker_permOp}
\leanok
Let $U_g$ and $V_g$ be permutation representations on finite-dimensional
spaces $\mathcal H$ and $\mathcal K$. If $v\in\mathcal H\otimes\mathcal K$
satisfies $(U_g\otimes V_g)v=v$, then
\[
 V_g\rho_{\mathcal K}=\rho_{\mathcal K}V_g,
 \qquad \rho_{\mathcal K}=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H}|v\rangle\langle v|.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The fixed-vector equation implies invariance of $|v\rangle\langle v|$
under conjugation by $U_g\otimes V_g$. The partial trace removes the
unitary $U_g$ and yields $V_g\rho_{\mathcal K}V_g^*=\rho_{\mathcal K}$.
Multiply on the right by $V_g$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Marginal symmetry of an excitation component]
\label{thm:excitation_component_marginal_symmetry}
\lean{Matrix.commute_partialTraceLeft_replicaExcitationComponent}
\leanok
\uses{thm:replica_excitation_preserves_fixed,thm:fixed_vector_marginal_symmetry}
Let $\Omega\in\mathcal H$, let $B\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}$, and put
$w=(R_B\otimes I_{\mathcal K})v$.
Suppose $\sigma B=B$ and $(U_\sigma\otimes V_\sigma)v=v$, where
$U_\sigma$ permutes the physical copies and $V_\sigma$ is a permutation
representation on $\mathcal K$. Then $V_\sigma$ commutes with
$\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H^{\otimes k}}|w\rangle\langle w|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
\uses{thm:replica_excitation_preserves_fixed,thm:fixed_vector_marginal_symmetry}
The stabilizer condition gives $(U_\sigma\otimes V_\sigma)w=w$.
Apply the preceding theorem to $w$.
\end{proof}

Both statements include empty registers and zero vectors. To obtain the
specific good-copy marginal in the merge-moment argument, one must also
select and regroup the relevant registers. The moment estimate itself is
a further assertion.
