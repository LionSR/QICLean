\section{Regional product density on the good copies}
\label{sec:replica_regional_density}

Let \(\Omega\in\mathbb C^Q\otimes\mathbb C^T\) be unit, let
\(B\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}\), and write \(G=B^c\) and \(m=|G|\).
For an arbitrary vector \(u\) with auxiliary register \(\mathcal K\), set
\(w=(R_B\otimes I_{\mathcal K})u\), where \(R_B\) is the exact physical
excitation projection. Enumerate \(G\) in its chosen finite-set enumeration.
These are the regional reduced matrices used in \cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw},
Section~7, equation (comparator:merge-moments).

\begin{theorem}[Actual regional density on the good copies]
\label{thm:replica_regional_product_density}
\lean{Matrix.partialTraceRight_replicaExcitationComponent_goodRegional_density}
\leanok
\uses{thm:replica_good_component,thm:replica_good_copy_auxiliary_density}
Write \(\rho_Q=\operatorname{Tr}_T|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\) and
\(\rho_{\mathcal K}(w)=\operatorname{Tr}_{(QT)^{\otimes k}}
|w\rangle\langle w|\). After regrouping the retained coordinates as
\(Q^{\otimes m}\otimes\mathcal K\), one has
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{T^{\otimes G}(QT)^{\otimes B}}
 |w\rangle\langle w|
 =\rho_Q^{\otimes m}\otimes\rho_{\mathcal K}(w).
\]
Neither \(u\) nor \(w\) need be normalized or nonzero. Zero copies and
empty good sets are included.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\leanok
\uses{thm:replica_good_component,thm:replica_good_copy_auxiliary_density}
The exact excitation factorization writes the coordinates of \(w\) as
\[
 w(q,t,b,c)=\left(\prod_{i\in G}\Omega(q_i,t_i)\right)\chi(b,c).
\]
Summing the rank-one density over \(b\) gives the marginal of \(\chi\),
which is the actual auxiliary marginal of \(w\). Summing over \(t\)
factors into one sum per good copy:
\[
 \sum_{t\in T^G}\prod_{i\in G}
 \Omega(q_i,t_i)\overline{\Omega(q'_i,t_i)}
 =\prod_{i\in G}(\rho_Q)_{q_iq'_i}.
\]
The chosen enumeration identifies this product with
\(\rho_Q^{\otimes m}\). Empty products have value one, so the same
calculation includes \(m=0\).
\end{proof}

Tracing unwanted auxiliary registers preserves this product structure.
The good-auxiliary permutation symmetry and the subsequent merge-moment
estimate are separate assertions.
