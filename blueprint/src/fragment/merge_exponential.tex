\section{Exponential expansion of merge deficits}
\label{sec:merge_exponential}

Let $G$ be a finite group and $X$ a finite coordinate set. Let
$\phi_Q,\phi_E,\phi_{QE}$ be permutation representations on $X$ satisfying
\[
 \phi_Q(g)\phi_E(h)=\phi_E(h)\phi_Q(g),\qquad
 \phi_{QE}(g)=\phi_Q(g)\phi_E(g)\quad(g,h\in G).
\]
Write $P_Q^\lambda,P_E^\mu,P_{QE}^\nu$ for their actual irreducible-label
projections and $d_\lambda,d_\mu,d_\nu$ for the irreducible dimensions.
Let $F_Q,F_E,F_{QE}$ be the logarithmic dimension observables and set
$D=F_Q+F_E-F_{QE}$. These are the merge deficits in
\cite[Section~7, equations \textup{(comparator:merge-decomposition)} and
\textup{(comparator:merge-moments)}]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.

\begin{theorem}[Exact exponential expansion]
\label{thm:exp_merge_deficit_eq_sum}
\lean{PermutationRepresentation.exp_mergeDeficit_eq_sum}
\leanok
For every $b\in\mathbb R$,
\[
 e^{bD}=\sum_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}
 \left(\frac{d_\lambda d_\mu}{d_\nu}\right)^b
 P_Q^\lambda P_E^\mu P_{QE}^\nu.
\]
This identity includes empty ambient coordinate sets and absent labels.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The separate label resolutions commute. Centrality of their projections
also gives commutation with the combined action and its label projections.
Hence the actual products $P_Q^\lambda P_E^\mu P_{QE}^\nu$ form a joint
orthogonal resolution. Its three marginals give the three logarithmic
dimension observables. Thus $D$ has value
$\log d_\lambda+\log d_\mu-\log d_\nu$ on each joint summand.
Compute the exponential on each summand. Positivity of all irreducible
dimensions identifies the coefficient with the stated ratio power.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact trace expansion]
\label{thm:re_trace_exp_merge_deficit_eq_sum}
\lean{PermutationRepresentation.re_trace_mul_exp_mergeDeficit_eq_sum}
\leanok
For every complex matrix $\rho$ on $X$ and every $b\in\mathbb R$,
\[
 \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(\rho e^{bD})
 =\sum_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}
   \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}
      (\rho P_Q^\lambda P_E^\mu P_{QE}^\nu)
   \left(\frac{d_\lambda d_\mu}{d_\nu}\right)^b.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
\uses{thm:exp_merge_deficit_eq_sum}
Insert the preceding expansion and distribute multiplication and trace
over the finite sums. The coefficients are real, so they factor out of
the real trace pairing.
\end{proof}

For a density matrix this identifies the exponential expectation with the
finite-label expression in
\cite[Section~5, equation \textup{(replicas:merge-moment)}]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
The invariance hypotheses and the numerical bound for that expectation
belong to the subsequent moment estimate.
