\section{The good auxiliary observable on a whole label}
\label{sec:good_auxiliary_label_compression}

The preceding whole-space comparison restricts to each actual whole-copy
label subspace. The restricted good-copy observable retains the whole
logarithmic dimension, up to the same bad-copy and binomial corrections.

\begin{theorem}[Good auxiliary bound on a whole label]
\label{thm:good_auxiliary_label_compression}
\lean{TensorPower.copyPerm_groupedGood_labelEntropy_compression}
\leanok
Let $C$ be a finite coordinate set, put $d=|C|$, and specify groups of $m$
good and $r$ bad copies with $m+r=k$. For the actual copy-permutation action
on $\mathbb C^{C^k}$, write $F_g$ for the good-copy logarithmic label
observable and $P_\lambda$ for the whole-copy central projection of an
irreducible label of dimension $d_\lambda$. Then $F_g$ commutes with
$P_\lambda$, and
\[
 \left(\log d_\lambda-r\log d-\log\binom{k}{r}\right)P_\lambda
       \le P_\lambda F_g P_\lambda.
\]
No occurrence assumption is imposed on $\lambda$. Empty coordinate sets
and zero copy groups are included, with the convention $\log0=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
\uses{thm:good_auxiliary_label_entropy}
Every permutation of the good copies is also a whole-copy permutation,
so it commutes with the whole-copy central projection. Summing the
central projections of the good-copy representation proves the stated
commutation. Compress the preceding whole-space upper bound for $F_w$
by $P_\lambda$. Since $P_\lambda$ is an orthogonal projection and
$F_wP_\lambda=(\log d_\lambda)P_\lambda$, the compressed inequality is
exactly the stated lower bound. This argument also includes
$P_\lambda=0$.
\end{proof}

This is the whole-label support step in
\cite[Section~7, equation \textup{(comparator:good-auxiliary)}]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
To apply it to an actual physical excitation component, the whole-copy
auxiliary label must be preserved under that physical projection.
