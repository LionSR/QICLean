\section{Two merge moments of an excitation component}
\label{sec:replica_two_merge_moment}

Let $Q,Y,V,C,R$ be finite coordinate sets, and let
$\Omega\in\mathbb C^Q\otimes\mathbb C^Y\otimes\mathbb C^V$ be unit.
Let $u$ belong to the $k$ physical copies together with $k$ copies of each
auxiliary register, and suppose that $u$ is fixed by simultaneous permutation
of the physical, $C$ and $R$ copy strings.
For $B\subseteq\{0,\ldots,k-1\}$ put $m=|B^c|$ and
$w=(R_B\otimes I)u$.
Let $\rho$ be the actual marginal of $|w\rangle\langle w|$ on
$(Q_{B^c}C_{B^c})\otimes(V_{B^c}R_{B^c})$.
Thus every middle physical coordinate and all bad-copy coordinates are traced out.

On this common space define
\[
 D_C=(F_Q+F_C-F_{QC})\otimes I,
 \qquad D_R=I\otimes(F_V+F_R-F_{VR}),
\]
using the actual good-copy permutation actions, and put
\[
 p_{QC}=(m+1)^{(|Q|\,|C|)^2},
 \qquad p_{VR}=(m+1)^{(|V|\,|R|)^2}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Actual two-deficit arithmetic-mean moment]
\label{thm:replica_two_merge_moment}
\lean{Matrix.replicaGoodPairMarginal_exp_sum_mergeDeficit_le}
\leanok
\uses{def:replica_good_pair_marginal,def:pair_merge_deficit}
For every real $a$ with $2a\leq1$,
\begin{align}
 \operatorname{Re}\tr\bigl(\rho e^{a(D_C+D_R)}\bigr)
 &\leq \frac{p_{QC}+p_{VR}}{2}\,\|w\|^2.
 \label{eq:replica_two_merge_moment}
\end{align}
No normalization or nonvanishing of $w$ is required. Zero copies and an
empty good set are included.
This is an auxiliary polynomial bound for the rough estimate in
\cite[Section~8]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{thm:weighted_trace_exponential,thm:replica_individual_pair_merge_moments}
The literal partial trace gives $\rho\geq0$, while $D_C,D_R$ are commuting
Hermitian matrices acting on separate tensor factors. The arithmetic-mean
trace estimate bounds the left side by
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{Re}\tr(\rho e^{2aD_C})
       +\operatorname{Re}\tr(\rho e^{2aD_R})}{2}.
\]
Since $2a\leq1$, Theorem~\ref{thm:replica_individual_pair_merge_moments}
bounds the two terms by $p_{QC}\|w\|^2$ and $p_{VR}\|w\|^2$,
respectively. Their arithmetic mean gives~(\ref{eq:replica_two_merge_moment}).
\end{proof}

The paper combines these moments by Cauchy--Schwarz. The arithmetic-mean
bound above is sufficient for this polynomial step; the physical spectral
restriction and inverse-filter estimates are subsequent arguments.
