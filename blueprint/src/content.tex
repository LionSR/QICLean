% The blueprint has six parts. Each part opens with a part file
% chapter/part_N_*.tex. Chapters are ordered by dependency as far as the
% order of Wolf's chapters allows; most remaining references to a later
% chapter start in Part I or in the Perron--Frobenius chapter. Parts I-III
% follow the order of M. Wolf, "Quantum Channels & Operations: A Guided
% Tour", Chapters 1-7; Part IV starts with Wolf's Chapter 8 on distances and
% entropies; Parts V and VI collect the tools for ground states of gapped
% local Hamiltonians, the one-dimensional area law, the two-dimensional area
% law and the PEPS approximation.
%
% The chNN_ prefix of a file name records where the file sat in the earlier
% Wolf-ordered layout; it is not the chapter number. Files and labels keep
% their names because docs/ and TNLean refer to them. A chapter is either a
% wrapper file that \input's its sections (the *_chapter.tex files and the
% older wrappers such as ch02_channels) or a file that carries its own
% \chapter heading, followed below by the files that continue it (for
% example the supporting-results sections at the end of a Wolf chapter).
%
%   I   States, positive maps and quantum channels
%        1 States, the Schmidt decomposition and Wigner's theorem
%        2 Positive maps, Kraus maps and partial traces
%        3 Choi matrices, Kraus representations and normal forms
%        4 Positive maps that are not completely positive, and
%          entanglement criteria
%        5 Duality for conic and semidefinite programs
%   II  Operator inequalities
%        6 Schwarz inequalities and multiplicative domains
%        7 Operator convexity, Jensen's inequality and Lieb concavity
%   III Spectra and long-time behaviour of channels
%        8 Fixed points and the Perron--Frobenius theorem
%        9 Peripheral spectrum, primitivity and spectral gaps
%       10 Fixed-point algebras and the asymptotic decomposition of channels
%       11 The quantum Wielandt bound (Sanz, Perez-Garcia, Wolf, Cirac 2010)
%       12 Dynamical semigroups and the GKSL theorem
%   IV  Entropy and probability
%       13 Trace distance, majorization and pinching
%       14 Von Neumann entropy and relative entropy
%       15 Strong subadditivity, mutual information and conditional entropy
%       16 Renyi entropies and truncated spectra
%       17 Trace exponentials and the matrix Bernstein inequality
%       18 Schur--Weyl duality and the labels of tensor powers
%       19 Random words with Poisson letter counts
%   V   Ground states of gapped local Hamiltonians
%       20 Polynomial filters for the ground projection
%       21 Gaussian and compactly supported spectral filters
%       22 Viable subspaces, nets and the detectability lemma
%   VI  Tools for the two-dimensional area law and PEPS approximation
%       23 Gaussian replacement of Schmidt sources
%       24 Projections, compressions and matrix means
%       25 Surprisal concentration and typical states
%       26 Conditional movement, phase and skew estimates
%       27 Replica components, restoring operators and good-copy densities
%       28 Common cutoffs for the labels of replicas
%       29 Replica entropy metrics and their upper bounds
%       30 Lower bounds and floors for replica metrics
%       31 Entropy and energy transport on symmetric replicas
%
% Chapter 22 keeps the viable-subspace, low-energy and trimming files of the
% former Chapter 4 together and in their former order.
