\part{States, positive maps and quantum channels}
\label{part:states_and_channels}

Finite-dimensional quantum states and the maps between them, following the
first four chapters of Wolf's lecture notes~\cite{Wolf2012Quantum}. This part
introduces states and the Schmidt decomposition, positive and completely
positive maps, the Choi and Kraus representations of quantum channels, the
positive maps that are not completely positive together with the entanglement
criteria they give, and duality for conic programs.
