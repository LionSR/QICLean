\section{Entropy of the selected Schmidt auxiliary state}

The selected Schmidt vector determines the entropy appearing in the
asymptotic auxiliary label dimension. Its entropy is that of the same
normalized spectral restriction used in the typical-set construction
of \cite[Section~8]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.

\begin{theorem}[Entropy after relabeling the selected Schmidt factor]
  \label{thm:selected_schmidt_auxiliary_entropy}
  \lean{Matrix.entropy_compressedTypicalPureState_equivFin_swap}
  \leanok
  Let $\Omega\in\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B$, and diagonalize
  $\rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_B|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$
  in an orthonormal basis $(e_i)$, with eigenvalues $(p_i)$.
  Choose a set $E$ of eigenvector indices with
  $z=\sum_{i\in E}p_i>0$. Write $\Pi_E$ for the corresponding
  spectral projection and put $\sigma=z^{-1}\Pi_E\rho_A\Pi_E$.
  Compress the selected factor to $\mathbb C^E$, normalize by
  $z^{-1/2}$, interchange the two factors, and relabel $E$ by
  $\{1,\ldots,|E|\}$. If $\psi\in\mathcal H_B\otimes\mathbb C^{|E|}$
  is this vector, then
  \[
    S\!\left(\operatorname{Tr}_B|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\right)
    =S(\sigma).
  \]
  No normalization of the original vector is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  \uses{thm:entropy_typical_density_spectrum}
  The auxiliary marginal of $\psi$ is diagonal with entries $p_i/z$
  for $i\in E$. Relabeling the finite index set preserves the sum
  defining its entropy. The normalized spectral restriction has the
  same selected eigenvalues, with additional zero eigenvalues on the
  orthogonal complement. Those zero eigenvalues contribute zero.
\end{proof}
