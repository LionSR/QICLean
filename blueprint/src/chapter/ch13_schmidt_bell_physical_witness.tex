\section{A physical test vector with the supplied auxiliary label}

The following consequence of the common cutoff estimate keeps the selected
state and auxiliary label fixed. The test vector is chosen before the matched
regional cutoffs. All limiting statements concern one fixed physical system,
as in \cite[Section~8, lines~332--381]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.

\begin{theorem}[A test vector for the supplied prevector]
    \label{thm:schmidt_bell_physical_witness}
    \lean{FiniteProduct.eventually_exists_suppliedSchmidtBell_physicalWitness}
    \uses{def:schmidt_bell_witness_coordinates,def:schmidt_bell_site_prevector}
    Let $\beta$ be a finite family of finite coordinate sets, let $\Omega$ be
    a unit vector on its product, and let $X$ be a set of physical sites.
    Fix a spectral selection $E$ of the reduced density on $X$, of mass
    $z>0$, and put $d=|E|$. The two auxiliary coordinate sets are $C=E$ and
    $R=[d]=\{0,\ldots,d-1\}$. Let $\psi$ be the corresponding normalized
    compressed selected vector on $X^c\times R$, and let $\Psi_k$ be its
    tensor power in exterior-site coordinates. Let $\ell_k$ be a supplied
    sequence of auxiliary labels. Suppose that, for all sufficiently large $k$,
    \begin{align*}
        q_k:=\|\Pi^{R}_{\ell_k}\Psi_k\|^2
        &\ge \frac{1}{2(k+1)^{d^2}}.
    \end{align*}
    Fix a finite nested list $\mathcal R$ of physical regions disjoint from
    $X$, and an arbitrary index set $S$. Let $b$ be the selected Bell vector
    on $X\times E$. Then every original and auxiliary coordinate set is
    nonempty and $\|b\|=1$.

    Write $\tau_k$ for the selected-Bell witness coordinates, $J_k$ for the
    actual insertion of $b^{\otimes k}$ in physical coordinates, and
    $\Pi_{\mathrm{ext},k}$ for simultaneous copy symmetry on the exterior
    sites. Put $P_k=J_k\Pi_{\mathrm{ext},k}J_k^*$, and let $v_k$ be the
    pullback through $\tau_k$ of the supplied-label prevector in
    Definition~\ref{def:schmidt_bell_site_prevector}.
    For all sufficiently large $k$, there is a vector $\Phi_k$ such that
    \begin{align*}
        \|\Phi_k\|&=1, \\
        P_k\Phi_k&=\Phi_k, \\
        \Pi^R_{\ell_k}\Phi_k&=\Phi_k, \\
        |\langle\Phi_k,P_kv_k\rangle|^2
        &\ge\frac{(z/d^2)^k}{4(k+1)^{d^2}}.
    \end{align*}
    The vector $\Phi_k$ is invariant under simultaneous copy permutations.

    This same vector has the following property for every $K\ge0$, every
    pair of families $U,Y:S\times\{0,\ldots,K-1\}\to2^V$, and all real
    $B_{\mathrm{sh}},B_{\mathrm{exc}}$. Suppose that
    $S_\Omega(D)\le B_{\mathrm{sh}}$ for every $D\in\mathcal R$, that
    $X$ is disjoint from every $U_{jg}\cup Y_{jg}$, and that each
    $Q\in\{U_{jg},U_{jg}\cup Y_{jg}\}$ has a region $D\in\mathcal R$
    comparable to $Q$ with
    \begin{align*}
        1+\log\dim\mathcal H_{D\triangle Q}&\le B_{\mathrm{exc}}.
    \end{align*}
    Set $a_k=k(B_{\mathrm{sh}}/z+B_{\mathrm{exc}})$. If
    $\Pi_{Q,\le a_k}$ denotes the original physical exterior cutoff,
    omitting $X$ and both auxiliary sites, then
    \begin{align*}
        \Pi_{U_{jg},\le a_k}\Phi_k&=\Phi_k, \\
        \Pi_{U_{jg}\cup Y_{jg},\le a_k}\Phi_k&=\Phi_k
    \end{align*}
    for all $j,g$. The vector and the eventual threshold are chosen before
    these matched families and bounds; no common threshold over different
    physical states or ambient systems is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \uses{thm:compressed_typical_common_retained_mass,thm:compressed_typical_common_bell_vector,thm:schmidt_bell_witness_range,thm:schmidt_bell_witness_auxiliary_label,thm:schmidt_bell_witness_exterior_cutoffs,thm:compressed_physical_matched_cutoff,thm:schmidt_bell_supplied_overlap}
    The regional exponential losses are eventually smaller than half the
    supplied inverse-polynomial mass. The common projection therefore has
    positive mass at least $1/[4(k+1)^{d^2}]$ before normalization. Apply the
    common Bell-vector construction with the given label and pull its vector
    back through $\tau_k$. This preserves its norm, symmetry, auxiliary label
    and Bell range. The exact Bell-pinning coefficient is
    $(\sqrt z/d)^k$, whose squared modulus is $(z/d^2)^k$. Self-adjointness
    of $P_k$ gives $\langle\Phi_k,P_kv_k\rangle=\langle\Phi_k,v_k\rangle$
    because $P_k\Phi_k=\Phi_k$.
    Each matched-cutoff estimate applies to the same common vector, and its
    coordinate pullback gives the two stated physical cutoff equations.
    Finally, a nonzero coordinate of the unit vector $\Omega$ and the positive
    selected mass give the asserted nonemptiness; the selected Bell vector
    has unit norm by its defining spectral embedding.
\end{proof}
