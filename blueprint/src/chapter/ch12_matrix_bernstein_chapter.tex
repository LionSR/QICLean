\chapter{Trace exponentials and the matrix Bernstein inequality}
\label{ch:matrix_bernstein}

The trace of the exponential of a Hermitian matrix controls the largest
eigenvalue of a sum of random matrices. This chapter proves trace inequalities
for exponentials of perturbed Hermitian matrices and for ordered products of
exponentials, Lieb's concavity of the trace exponential, and from them the
matrix Bernstein inequality for sums of independent bounded random Hermitian
matrices~\cite{Tropp2012User}.
