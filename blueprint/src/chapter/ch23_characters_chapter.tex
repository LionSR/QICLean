\chapter{Character projectors and semi-regular representations}
\label{ch:character_projectors}

This chapter develops the isotypic decomposition of a finite-dimensional
complex representation of a finite group from its characters alone. The
character projectors are built without a choice of basis, and weighted sums of
them give the operator $\Delta$ of Schuch, Cirac, and P\'erez-Garc\'ia, its
inverse, and its fourth root. A representation is semi-regular when it
contains every irreducible representation; this happens exactly when its group
operators are linearly independent. For unitary representations the
decomposition is orthonormal and agrees with the decomposition of the star
algebra generated by the group operators. The last section collects elementary
facts on finite groups and matrices used alongside these results.
