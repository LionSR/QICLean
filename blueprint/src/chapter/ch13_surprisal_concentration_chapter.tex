\chapter{Surprisal concentration and typical states}
\label{ch:surprisal_concentration}

The surprisal of an eigenvalue $p$ of a density matrix is $-\log p$. For a
marginal of a gapped ground state, sampled with the eigenvalues as
probabilities, the surprisal concentrates around the von Neumann entropy of the
marginal~\cite[Lemma~3.1]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. This chapter proves this
concentration, its analogue for independent copies of a state, and the
properties of the typical states obtained by keeping the eigenvalues whose
surprisal lies in a window around the entropy.
