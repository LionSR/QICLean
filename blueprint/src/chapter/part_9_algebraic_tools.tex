\part{Algebraic tools}
\label{part:algebraic_tools}

Statements about finite index sets, matrices and finite groups that the
earlier parts and their applications use repeatedly: sums and products over
finite index sets and finite Kronecker products, flags and word algebras,
unitary congruence and groups of matrices, monomial operators, character
projectors of finite groups, projective representations with the scalar
cohomology of their cocycles, scalar three-cocycles, and elementary
perturbation estimates.
