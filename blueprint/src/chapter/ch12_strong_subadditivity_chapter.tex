\chapter{Strong subadditivity, mutual information and conditional entropy}
\label{ch:strong_subadditivity}

The von Neumann entropy of three systems obeys strong subadditivity,
$S(ABC)+S(B)\le S(AB)+S(BC)$. This chapter proves it, characterizes its
equality case, and derives the nonnegativity and monotonicity of mutual
information and of conditional mutual information, the continuity of
conditional entropy, and the entropy identities for the regions of a pure state
on finitely many sites, together with bounds on classical mutual information
and on the operator Schmidt rank. It closes with the root fidelity, Uhlmann's
theorem and the bound of the relative entropy by the fidelity.
