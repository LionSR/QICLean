\section{Coefficients of unions of finite sets}

Grouping finite inclusion--exclusion by the union of the selected windows
produces the coefficients below. This gives a combinatorial ingredient
for an interaction-label estimate related to the cluster argument
of Arad--Landau--Vazirani--Vidick~\cite[lines 1935--1968]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.
The obstructions to the printed spatial estimate are recorded
in~\cite{gap:alvv16_cluster_approximation}.

\begin{lemma}[A bound for coefficients with a prescribed union]
    \label{thm:cluster_union_coefficient_bound}
    \lean{Finset.norm_sum_powerset_biUnion_sign_le_two_pow}
    \leanok

    Let $W$ be a finite family of finite subsets of a set $X$. Suppose
    there is an injective map $\mu:W\to X$ such that $\mu(I)\in I$ for
    every $I\in W$. For a finite subset $U\subseteq X$, put
    \begin{align}
        c_U
        &=\sum_{\substack{\varnothing\ne\mathcal F\subseteq W\\
              \bigcup\mathcal F=U}}
          (-1)^{|\mathcal F|+1}.
        \label{eq:cluster_union_coefficient}
    \end{align}
    Then $|c_U|\leq2^{|U|}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Put $V_U=\{I\in W:I\subseteq U\}$. The restriction of $\mu$ is an
    injection from $V_U$ into $U$, so $|V_U|\leq|U|$. Every family
    contributing to~(\ref{eq:cluster_union_coefficient}) is a subset of
    $V_U$. Its number is therefore at most $2^{|V_U|}\leq2^{|U|}$.
    Each summand has absolute value one, and the triangle inequality
    proves the result.
\end{proof}

For windows that are intervals of one fixed positive length, the first
label gives the required injective choice. The coefficient bound concerns
only the finite family and does not require any operator or norm estimate
for a Hamiltonian.
