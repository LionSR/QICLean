\chapter{Random words with Poisson letter counts}
\label{ch:poisson_words}

The Poissonized word law at time $t$ gives a word $w$ over an alphabet of $N$
letters the probability $e^{-Nt}t^{|w|}/|w|!$. Under this law the numbers of
occurrences of the letters are independent Poisson variables with mean $t$, and
given these numbers every order of the letters is equally likely. This chapter
computes the law of such words under concatenation, insertion, thinning and
relabelling, bounds the growth of observables along them, and sums
stretched-exponential kernels over the balls of a metric space. It closes with
the decay of products of contractions along a Poissonized word, which gives the
decay of excited states in the area-law argument of~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
