\chapter{Projective representations and scalar group cohomology}
\label{ch:projective_cohomology}

A projective representation multiplies only up to a scalar factor system, and
associativity makes that factor system a two-cocycle with values in the
nonzero complex numbers. This chapter identifies these cocycles and their
classes with degree-two group cohomology for the trivial action, compares
complex and unit-circle coefficients for finite groups, and treats the
restriction of classes to subgroups. It then constructs cocycle-twisted
regular representations, develops the generalized cocycles of a group acting
on a set of labels, following Franco-Rubio, Bochniak and
Cirac~\cite{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}, and treats representations that are
projective up to complex conjugation, as they arise from time reversal and
reflection~\cite{Cirac2021Matrix}.
