\chapter{Channel Representations and Normal Forms}\label{ch:channel_representations}

This chapter opens with the Choi--Jamio\l{}kowski foundations -- the
maximally entangled state, the Choi matrix, and the Choi criterion for
complete positivity -- and the no-information-without-disturbance theorem
that follows directly from them. It then collects the remaining
representation material for finite-dimensional quantum channels from
\cite[Chapter~2]{Wolf2012Quantum}: Kraus representation theorems,
Stinespring and Naimark dilations, ordered completely positive maps,
Radon--Nikodym and open-system representations, trace-pairing expansions,
SVD and Lorentz normal forms, and the channel determinant. These results
are important for the later channel theory, but they are not prerequisites
for the matrix-product-state Fundamental Theorem.
