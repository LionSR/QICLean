\section{Regional entropy under a change of pure state}

The continuity estimate for the entropy of a fixed region involves the
dimension of that region. The trace distance may be taken on the complete
finite system, by contraction under the partial trace.

\begin{theorem}[Continuity of regional entropy]
  \label{thm:finite_product_entropy_continuity}
  \lean{FiniteProduct.abs_entropy_sub_le_sqrt_traceDistance}
  Let $V$ be a finite set, with finite coordinate sets $\mathcal B_v$, and
  let $\mathcal H_R=\mathbb C^{\prod_{v\in R}\mathcal B_v}$ for $R\subseteq V$.
  For unit vectors $\Omega,\Phi\in\mathcal H_V$, write
  $\rho_\Omega=|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$,
  $\rho_\Phi=|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|$, and
  $\delta=\tfrac12\|\rho_\Omega-\rho_\Phi\|_1$.
  Then every region $R\subseteq V$ satisfies
  \begin{align*}
    |S_\Omega(R)-S_\Phi(R)|
    \le 4\sqrt\delta\,\log(e\dim\mathcal H_R).
  \end{align*}
  This is the regional form of Lemma~2.1 used in the scanner argument
  of \cite[Section~9]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. The two vectors and the region
  are supplied to the estimate; no further choice is made.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{cor:entropy_mutual_information_continuity,lem:trace_distance_jordan}
  Split the original coordinates into the region and its complement.
  The two regional densities are positive semidefinite with trace one.
  Apply the square-root continuity estimate to these densities. Partial
  trace contracts their trace distance, and relabeling the original
  coordinates leaves the full trace distance unchanged. The regional
  dimension is positive because its density has trace one, so the
  logarithmic factor is nonnegative and the stated bound follows.
\end{proof}
