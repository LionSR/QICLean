\section{Exact moments of independent finite copies}

The following finite identity underlies the independent-copy moment
calculation in \cite[Section~8]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. For real weights
$p_i$ on a finite set, put $M_p(u)=\sum_i p_i e^{-u\log p_i}$, where
$\log p=\log|p|$ for $p\ne0$ and $\log0=0$. In its probabilistic
application the weights are nonnegative. A zero weight contributes zero.

\begin{theorem}[Product surprisal moment]
  \label{thm:surprisal_product_moment}
  \lean{Entropy.surprisalMoment_pi}
  \leanok
  For every integer $k\geq0$ and every real $u$, define
  $p^{(k)}(x)=\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}p_{x_j}$. Then
  $M_{p^{(k)}}(u)=M_p(u)^k$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  A configuration with zero product weight contributes zero on both
  sides. For every remaining configuration, the logarithm of the
  product is the sum of the logarithms, so its exponential factors.
  Distribute the finite sums over the resulting product. For zero
  copies the unique empty configuration has weight one.
\end{proof}
