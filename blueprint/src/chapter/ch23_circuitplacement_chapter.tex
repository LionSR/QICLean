\chapter{Placement of circuits and asymptotic preparation}
\label{ch:circuitplacement}

This chapter collects three complements to the chapters on sites and circuits.
A product of gates on an open chain placed at arbitrary sites of a longer open
chain is again a product of gates on neighbouring sites, and a circuit that acts
cleanly with a zero workspace stays clean when it is placed in a larger
register with a common workspace. A monomial matrix placed along an injection of
sites is monomial. Finally, the trace norm of a difference of two pure states is
bounded by their overlap, which turns overlap estimates into the trace-norm
convergence of an asymptotic preparation with measurements.
