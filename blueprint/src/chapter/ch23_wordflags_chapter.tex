\chapter{Flags, word algebras, power sums and idempotent families}
\label{ch:wordflags}

This chapter collects finite-dimensional linear algebra about families of
matrices and their words: block-triangular forms along an invariant flag, the
free algebra on finitely many letters and its nilpotent modules, the matching
of two composition series in a modular lattice, the recovery of finite
multisets from power sums and of nilpotent and semisimple parts from trace
powers, continuous families of idempotents with their range frames, and
several finite-cycle and rank statements.
