\part{Ground states of gapped local Hamiltonians}
\label{part:gapped_ground_states}

Analytic tools for the ground states of local Hamiltonians with a spectral gap.
They enter Hastings' proof of the one-dimensional area
law~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}, the area law and algorithms of Arad, Kitaev,
Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite{Arad2013AreaLaw,Arad2016RigorousRG}, the
polynomial-time algorithm of Landau, Vazirani and
Vidick~\cite{Landau2013PolynomialTime}, and the two-dimensional results of the
last part. This part constructs polynomial, Gaussian and compactly supported
filters that approximate the projection onto the ground space, and proves the
estimates on viable subspaces, nets and the detectability lemma used by the
algorithms.
