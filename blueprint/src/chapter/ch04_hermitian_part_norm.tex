\section{Hermitian approximations in operator norm}
\label{sec:hermitian_part_norm}

The Hermitian part of a matrix $B$ is $(B+B^*)/2$. Its use preserves an
operator-norm approximation to a Hermitian target. This elementary estimate
supports the approximating Hamiltonian in the efficient-filter construction
of Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite[lines 1262--1268 and 1412--1420]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.

\begin{lemma}[Contractivity of the Hermitian part]
    \label{lem:hermitian_part_norm}
    \lean{Matrix.norm_selfAdjointPart_le,
        Matrix.IsHermitian.norm_sub_selfAdjointPart_le}
    \leanok
    For every finite square complex matrix $B$,
    \begin{align}
        \|(B+B^*)/2\|\leq\|B\|.
    \end{align}
    If $H=H^*$, then
    \begin{align}
        \|H-(B+B^*)/2\|\leq\|H-B\|.
    \end{align}
    These norms are the Hilbert-space operator norms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    The triangle inequality and $\|B^*\|=\|B\|$ prove the first estimate.
    The Hermitian-part map is real-linear and fixes $H$. Apply its
    contractivity to $H-B$ to obtain the second estimate.
\end{proof}
