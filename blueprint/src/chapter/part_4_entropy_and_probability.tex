\part{Entropy and probability}
\label{part:entropy_and_probability}

Distances and entropies of quantum states, and the probabilistic tools used
later in the volume. This part proves the basic properties of the trace
distance, the von Neumann and relative entropies and the fidelity
\cite[Chapter~8]{Wolf2012Quantum}, strong subadditivity of the entropy, bounds
relating R\'enyi entropies to truncated spectra, the matrix Bernstein
inequality for sums of independent random matrices, Schur--Weyl duality for
tensor powers, and estimates for random words whose letters arrive at
independent Poisson times.
