\part{Tools for the two-dimensional area law and PEPS approximation}
\label{part:area_law_tools}

The two-dimensional area law for gapped local
Hamiltonians~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} and the approximation of gapped ground
states on the square grid by projected entangled pair states (PEPS) of
polynomial bond dimension~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} rest on estimates
that do not refer to the lattice; a PEPS is a tensor network with one tensor on
each site of the grid. This part proves these estimates: Gaussian replacements
of Schmidt sources, inequalities for projections and matrix means, the
concentration of the surprisal of a marginal of a gapped ground state, three
conditional operator estimates, and the analysis of symmetric replicas, that
is, of several copies of a state on which the symmetric group acts by permuting
the copies.
