\chapter{Gaussian replacement of Schmidt sources}
\label{ch:gaussian_sources}

A Schmidt source is the vector $\sum_a\sqrt{\lambda_a}\,|a,a\rangle$ given by
Schmidt weights $\lambda_a$. The PEPS construction
of~\cite[Theorem~5.2]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} replaces each Schmidt source by
a random operator with independent complex Gaussian coefficients whose
expectation is the source. This chapter proves the second-moment and trace-norm
estimates for these replacements, bounds the expected trace error of the
resulting physical state, and places all samples on one probability space.
