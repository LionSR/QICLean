\chapter{Von Neumann entropy and relative entropy}\label{ch:entropy}

This chapter defines the von Neumann entropy of a density matrix and the
relative entropy of two density matrices, following
\cite[Chapter~8]{Wolf2012Quantum}, and proves their basic properties: bounds
by the logarithm of the rank, the calculus of inverse square roots on the
support used for relative entropies, the Petz recovery formula for a partial
trace, and data processing under local channels.
