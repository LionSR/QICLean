\chapter{R\'enyi entropies and truncated spectra}
\label{ch:renyi}

Discarding the smallest eigenvalues of a density matrix changes it by the
discarded weight in trace norm. This chapter bounds that weight by the von
Neumann and R\'enyi entropies, proves the converse lower bounds on the rank
needed to approximate a state to a given accuracy, and treats smoothed R\'enyi
entropies. The chapter opens with the truncation of the spectrum of a positive
matrix shifted by a multiple of the identity.
