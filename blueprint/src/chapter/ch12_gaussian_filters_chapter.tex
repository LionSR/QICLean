\chapter{Gaussian and compactly supported spectral filters}
\label{ch:gaussian_filters}

Averaging the time evolution generated by a Hamiltonian against a Gaussian
weight suppresses the matrix elements between the ground space and the excited
states while keeping the evolution almost local. This chapter proves the
estimates on these Gaussian filters, constructs a filter whose frequency
support is compact and whose time kernel still decays faster than any
polynomial~\cite[Lemma~4.2]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, and approximates the ground
projection of a gapped Hamiltonian by powers of a positive contraction.
