\chapter{Common cutoffs for the labels of replicas}
\label{ch:label_cutoffs}

The permutations of $k$ copies decompose their tensor power into sectors
labelled by the irreducible representations of the symmetric group. This
chapter chooses one cutoff on these labels that serves every region of a
compressed typical state, bounds the mass that the cutoff discards, and proves
the entropy and dimension bounds for the labels of the good and bad copies used
in~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
