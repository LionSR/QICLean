\section{An explicit power for the energy-estimation space}

The energy-estimation space in~\cite[Figure \texttt{fig:algo1}, line 1631]
{Arad2016RigorousRG} is a separate untrimmed operator-word image. Its
accuracy can be chosen without adding collective trims to that image.

\begin{lemma}[A logarithmic power of the same factor family]
    \label{lem:untrimmed_tensor_family_ceil_log}
    \lean{Real.word_filter_error_ceil_log_le,
        Submodule.IsSpectralProjector.viability_word_power_ceil_log}
    \leanok
    Retain the spectral filter, low target $T$, cutoff separation and finite
    tensor decomposition $K=\sum_{i\in I} A_i\otimes B_i$ from
    Lemma~\ref{thm:spectral_operator_word_powers}. Suppose
    $0\le\Delta\le1/4$. Let $S$ be $\delta$-viable for $T$, with
    $\delta\le1/2$. For any $\eta>0$, put
    \[
       p=\max\left\{0,\left\lceil4\log(4/\eta)\right\rceil\right\}+1,\qquad
       V=\operatorname{span}\{A_{i_1}\cdots A_{i_p}s:
                  i_1,\ldots,i_p\in I,\ s\in S\}.
    \]
    Then $V$ is $\eta$-viable for the same target and
    \[
       \dim V\le |I|^p\dim S.
    \]
    The target, filter and factors are fixed before the accuracy and
    power are chosen.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:spectral_operator_word_powers}
    The existing word-power estimate gives error
    $\Delta^p/(1-\delta)^2$ on this exact image and the stated dimension.
    Since $\Delta\le1/4\le3/4$, one has
    \[
       \Delta^p\le(3/4)^p\le e^{-p/4}\le\eta/4,
       \qquad (1-\delta)^2\ge1/4.
    \]
    Division gives the required accuracy.
\end{proof}

This is an explicit sufficient power. The source's printed power,
the generated local soft Hamiltonian, its compression and the energy
comparison remain separate conclusions.
