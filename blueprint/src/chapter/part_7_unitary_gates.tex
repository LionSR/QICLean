\part{Unitary gates}
\label{part:unitary_gates}

Unitary matrices indexed by an arbitrary finite set, with no tensor-product
structure on the index set. This part proves the matrix statements used to
write a unitary as a product of elementary factors: the Givens decomposition
into two-level rotations and phases, the commutator identity for controlled
elements, the unitary dilation of a partial isometry, and the exact
amplification of a uniform success probability by two reflections.
