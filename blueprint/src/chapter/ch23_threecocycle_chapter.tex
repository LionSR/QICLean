\chapter{Scalar three-cocycles and L-symbols}
\label{ch:threecocycle}

A scalar three-cocycle of a group $G$ is a function
$\omega\colon G^3\to\C^\times$ satisfying the multiplicative three-cocycle
equation; up to the gauge action of scalar two-cochains it is a class in the
degree-three group cohomology of $G$ with coefficients in $\C^\times$ and the
trivial action. This chapter develops these cocycles and their gauge classes,
the cyclic invariants that detect nontrivial classes, and the
$L$-symbols of a group action on a set, families of scalars whose
compatibility with $\omega$ trivializes the cocycle along the stabilizers of
the action. For transitive actions the $L$-symbols are classified by
two-cocycles of a stabilizer. The chapter closes with the complete
computation for the cyclic groups and the Klein four-group, following
Garre-Rubio, Lootens and Moln\'ar~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}, Garre-Rubio
and Schuch~\cite{GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall}, and Franco-Rubio,
Bochniak and Cirac~\cite{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}.
