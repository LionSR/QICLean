\chapter{Perturbation estimates and operators on fibers}
\label{ch:perturbation_estimates}

This chapter collects finite-dimensional estimates used in perturbation
arguments. Ordered products close to an idempotent differ from its powers by
a telescoping sum, and their traces differ only to second order when the
perturbations vanish after compression by the idempotent. Square roots in a
C$^*$-algebra are Lipschitz above a positive scalar floor, and a Sylvester
equation with positive coefficients has a bounded solution independent of the
size of the coefficients. Range projections can be written through Gram
inverses supported on a subspace, and the defect of a product of two
projections from a third is controlled by sectorwise errors. Operators applied
independently on the fibers of a finite product keep their norms, kernels,
ranges, projections and gaps.
