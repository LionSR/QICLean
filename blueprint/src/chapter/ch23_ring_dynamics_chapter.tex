\chapter{Dynamics on the ring}
\label{ch:ring_dynamics}

On the ring of $N$ sites, the Lieb--Robinson bound of Hastings and Koma for
nearest-neighbour interactions has velocity $8e^a$ for the spatial weight
$e^{-a d}$, with constants independent of the ring length and of the dimension
of the sites. For a translation-invariant interaction this bound is the
short-time input of the locality estimates for Gaussian-filtered operators and
for regularized negative-frequency operators.
