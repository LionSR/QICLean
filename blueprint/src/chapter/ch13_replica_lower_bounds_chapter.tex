\chapter{Lower bounds and floors for replica metrics}
\label{ch:replica_lower_bounds}

Chapter~\ref{ch:replica_metrics}: inverse estimates for the replica metrics on
the excitation components, lower comparisons of the metrics in physical
coordinates for every regional cut, and polynomial floors for the metrics on
the symmetric subspace of complete copies~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
