\chapter{Product vectors, placed configurations and site oscillation}
\label{ch:siteconfig}

This chapter collects facts about a finite system of sites that are used with
the supported operators and placements of Chapter~\ref{ch:sites}. Product
vectors are mapped to product vectors by product operators, and operators
placed on disjoint sets of sites have explicit matrix elements and act on
configurations placed on disjoint windows by explicit amplitudes. Expectations
of unitaries are stable under changes of the vector. Finally, the oscillation
of an operator at one site, with an untouched finite spectator, controls the
error of averaging over the sites.
