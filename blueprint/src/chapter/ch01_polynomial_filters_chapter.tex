\chapter{Polynomial filters for the ground projection}
\label{ch:polynomial_filters}

A polynomial in a gapped Hamiltonian that equals one at the ground energy and
is small on the rest of the spectrum approximates the projection onto the
ground space. This chapter proves the estimates on such polynomials used by
Arad, Kitaev, Landau, Vazirani and
Vidick~\cite{Arad2013AreaLaw,Arad2016RigorousRG}: interpolation of polynomial
coefficients, truncation of the high-energy part of a Hamiltonian, the
expansion of a polynomial in noncommuting local terms over ordered words and
the operator Schmidt rank of its coefficients, and Chebyshev filters. It ends
with the overlap estimates of Landau, Vazirani and
Vidick~\cite{Landau2013PolynomialTime}.
