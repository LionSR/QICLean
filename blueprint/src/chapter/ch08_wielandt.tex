\chapter{Wielandt Bound}\label{ch:wielandt}

This chapter proves the quantum Wielandt bounds of
\cite{Sanz2010Quantum}. For a primitive normalized tensor, it compares the
uniform vector-spreading index $q(\E_A)$ with the first exact word length
$\iota(A)$ satisfying $S_{\iota(A)}(A)=\MN{D}$ and proves
$\iota(A)\le(D^2-\krausrk(A)+1)D^2$. The cumulative bound
$T_{D^2}(A)=\MN{D}$ is an intermediate step in the proof of Lemma~1.
