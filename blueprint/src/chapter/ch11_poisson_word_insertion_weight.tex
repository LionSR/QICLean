\section{The insertion-time weight identity}
\label{sec:poisson_word_insertion_weight}

For a finite alphabet $I$, put $N=|I|$ and retain the singleton word
weights $w_t(m)=e^{-Nt}t^m/m!$ from
Definition~\ref{def:poisson_word_measure}.  The following integration
identity supplies the scalar calculation for inserting a marked letter
between a prefix and a suffix.  It is a derived identity for the finite-word
model motivated by the occurrence-order products in
\emph{A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap},
\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, \texttt{09-amplification.tex}, lines 49--54,
and its integration over update times on lines 203--209; it is a derived
identity rather than a theorem stated in that manuscript.
% Source revision: adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a.

\begin{lemma}[Integration of prefix and suffix weights]
\label{lem:poisson_word_insertion_weight}
\lean{PoissonWord.continuous_weight_ofReal,
PoissonWord.measurable_weight_ofReal,
PoissonWord.weight_mul_weight_nonneg,
PoissonWord.intervalIntegrable_weight_mul_weight,
PoissonWord.integral_weight_mul_weight,
PoissonWord.lintegral_weight_mul_weight}
\leanok
\uses{def:poisson_word_measure}
For every $T\geq0$ and $m,n\in\N$,
\begin{align}
 \int_0^T w_s(m)w_{T-s}(n)\,ds
 &=w_T(m+n+1).
 \label{eq:poisson_word_insertion_weight}
\end{align}
The integrand is nonnegative and integrable.  Equivalently, the same
identity holds as a nonnegative Lebesgue integral on $(0,T]$ after viewing
the weights in $[0,\infty]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
At $T=0$, both sides vanish.  For $T>0$, the beta integral and its relation
to the gamma function at positive integers give
\begin{align}
 \int_0^T s^m(T-s)^n\,ds
 &=T^{m+n+1}\frac{m!\,n!}{(m+n+1)!}.
 \label{eq:poisson_word_insertion_beta}
\end{align}
On the integration interval, the two exponential factors multiply to
$e^{-NT}$.  Factoring out $e^{-NT}/(m!n!)$ proves the identity.
Continuity proves integrability; nonnegativity identifies the real and
nonnegative integrals.  Writing $s_+=\max(s,0)$ extends the two factors
to continuous functions $s\mapsto w_{s_+}(m)$ and
$s\mapsto w_{(T-s)_+}(n)$ on the real line.  These extensions agree with
the original weights on $[0,T]$.
\end{proof}

The identity includes zero time and allows $I=\varnothing$.  It does not
construct a clock process or a coupling of event times.
