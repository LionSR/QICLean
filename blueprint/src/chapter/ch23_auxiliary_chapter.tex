\chapter{Auxiliary estimates and identities}
\label{ch:auxiliary_estimates}

This chapter collects elementary statements used throughout the area-law and
approximation arguments: contractions in the operator norm and the cyclic
insertion map, entropies of flat densities, coordinate inclusions and isometric
frames, a weak Perron--Frobenius theorem, counting of frequency vectors and the
scalar recovery of word coefficients, explicit thresholds for elementary limits,
and a few facts about graphs.
