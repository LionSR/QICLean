\chapter{States, the Schmidt decomposition and Wigner's theorem}\label{ch:deconstructing_quantum}

Following Wolf's opening chapter \cite[Chapter~1]{Wolf2012Quantum}, this
chapter sets up states as density operators and observables as Hermitian
matrices under the trace pairing. It proves the Schmidt decomposition of a
bipartite pure state, purification and quantum steering, and Wigner's theorem:
a bijection of the pure states that preserves transition probabilities is
conjugation by a unitary, possibly composed with the transpose. The spectral
facts behind these results, from trace-power identities to the classification
of spectrum-preserving maps, and a few matrix facts used throughout the volume
close the chapter.
