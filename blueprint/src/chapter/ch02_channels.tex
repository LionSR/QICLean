\chapter{Positive maps, Kraus maps and partial traces}\label{ch:channels}

This chapter develops positive and completely positive maps between matrix
algebras and quantum channels, following
\cite[Chapters~1 and~2]{Wolf2012Quantum}. A map given by Kraus operators is
completely positive, and it is a channel when it also preserves the trace. The
chapter treats maps between rectangular matrix algebras and their trace
adjoints, maps acting on one tensor factor, the partial trace, the transfer map
of a finite family of matrices, and the extension of completely positive maps
from operator systems.
