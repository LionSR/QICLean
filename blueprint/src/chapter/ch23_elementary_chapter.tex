\chapter{Elementary finite constructions}
\label{ch:elementary_finite_constructions}

This chapter collects elementary facts about finite index sets, ordered
products, finite tensor products of matrices, representations of finite
products of matrix algebras, coalgebras, directed walks, and matrix-valued
functions of one real variable. They are used throughout the later chapters
as bookkeeping steps; each is stated here with the hypotheses it actually
needs.
