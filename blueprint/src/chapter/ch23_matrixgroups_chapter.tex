\chapter{Unitary congruence, Kronecker factors, and groups of matrices}
\label{ch:matrixgroups}

This chapter collects finite-dimensional matrix facts about unitaries and
Kronecker products: scalar and unitary factors of Kronecker products, the swap
matrix and the realignment of a bipartite matrix, the congruence normal forms
$x=S^T\Lambda S$ of symmetric and skew-symmetric unitaries, path connectedness
and compactness of groups of matrices, a lifting theorem for paths that are
continuous modulo local unitaries, and star-isomorphisms of full matrix
algebras.
