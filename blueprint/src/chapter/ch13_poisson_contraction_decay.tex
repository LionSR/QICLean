\section{Poissonized excited-state decay}
\label{sec:poisson_contraction_decay}

Let $\mathcal H=\C^d$ and let $\mathcal K$ be an arbitrary finite-dimensional
spectator space.  For a finite label set $I$, write $N=|I|$ and let
$D_w$ be the chronological square-root product of
Definition~\ref{def:contraction_word}.  The probability measure $\mu_t$
is the explicit word law of Definition~\ref{def:poisson_word_measure}.
The following estimate is the finite-word probability form of the excited-state
bound in \emph{A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap},
\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, \texttt{09-amplification.tex}, lines 237--253.

\begin{definition}[Word energy with a spectator]
    \label{def:poisson_contraction_energy}
    \lean{Matrix.poissonContractionWordEnergy}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:contraction_word, def:poisson_word_measure}
    For $\xi\in\mathcal H\otimes\mathcal K$ define the nonnegative observable
    \begin{align}
        F_\xi(w)&=\|(D_w\otimes I_{\mathcal K})\xi\|^2.
        \label{eq:poisson_contraction_energy}
    \end{align}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exponential decay under the word law]
    \label{thm:poisson_contraction_decay}
    \lean{Matrix.poissonContractionWordEnergy_integrable_and_le,
        Matrix.poissonContractionWord_excitedProjection_decay}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:poisson_contraction_energy}
    Let $\Omega\in\mathcal H$, set $P_\Omega=|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$,
    and assume $k_i\le I$, $k_i\Omega=0$, and
    $\sum_i k_i\ge g(I-P_\Omega)$ for $g\in\R$.
    If $\xi\in\Omega^\perp\otimes\mathcal K$, then $F_\xi$ is integrable
    and, for every $t\ge0$,
    \begin{align}
        \int F_\xi\,d\mu_t
        &\le e^{-gt}\|\xi\|^2.
        \label{eq:poisson_contraction_decay}
    \end{align}
    If $\|\Omega\|=1$, then $P_\Omega$ is an orthogonal projection,
    and in particular this holds for
    $\xi=((I-P_\Omega)\otimes I_{\mathcal K})\zeta$ and arbitrary
    $\zeta\in\mathcal H\otimes\mathcal K$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:contraction_spectator_decay, thm:contraction_spectator_projection,
        thm:contraction_spectator_large_gap, thm:poisson_word_decay}
    Suppose first that $g\le N$.  The finite-word bound gives
    $\sum_{|w|=m}F_\xi(w)\le(N-g)^m\|\xi\|^2$.
    The weighted summation theorem proves integrability and gives
    \begin{align}
        \int F_\xi\,d\mu_t
        &\le e^{-Nt}\sum_{m\ge0}\frac{t^m(N-g)^m}{m!}\|\xi\|^2
         =e^{-gt}\|\xi\|^2.
        \label{eq:poisson_contraction_series}
    \end{align}
    If $g>N$, the one-step gap inequality forces $\xi=0$, so both sides
    vanish.  The complementary ground projection puts every physical
    coordinate slice in $\Omega^\perp$, proving the last assertion.
\end{proof}

The result permits an empty label set, time zero, and an empty spectator.
There is no spectator-dimension factor.  The source application has
$0\le k_i\le I$ and a positive gap; the algebraic estimate above also
allows the displayed weaker hypotheses.  Identifying $\mu_t$ with the
chronological events of independent rate-one clocks is a separate formal
bridge.  This result does not provide the coupled-clock locality estimate or the
full amplification theorem.
