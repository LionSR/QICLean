\chapter{Peripheral spectrum, primitivity and spectral gaps}
\label{ch:spectral}

This chapter studies the peripheral spectrum of finite-dimensional quantum
channels: the eigenvalues of unit modulus that survive the ergodic averaging
of Chapter~\ref{ch:qpf}, following~\cite[Chapter~6]{Wolf2012Quantum}. For a
finite Kraus map, the peripheral eigenvalues form a finite cyclic group. A
map is primitive when one is its only peripheral eigenvalue; the chapter
identifies primitivity with period one and proves that the transfer map of a
primitive family has a spectral gap on the complement of its fixed points.
