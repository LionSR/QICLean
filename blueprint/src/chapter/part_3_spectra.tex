\part{Spectra and long-time behaviour of channels}
\label{part:spectra}

The spectrum of a channel governs its powers and its long-time behaviour,
following \cite[Chapters~6 and~7]{Wolf2012Quantum}. This part proves the
mean-ergodic theorem and the Perron--Frobenius theory of positive maps, the
structure of the peripheral spectrum, the decomposition of the algebra of fixed
points, the quantum Wielandt bound on the length of the products of Kraus
operators needed to span the full matrix algebra, and the
Gorini--Kossakowski--Sudarshan--Lindblad form of the generator of a semigroup
of channels.
