\chapter{Trace distance, majorization and pinching}
\label{ch:trace_distance}

The trace norm of the difference of two density matrices measures how well the
two states can be told apart by a measurement. This chapter proves the
properties of the trace norm used throughout the volume, including its duality
with the operator norm and its behaviour under pinching, the measurement that
forgets its outcome. It also proves Birkhoff's theorem that doubly stochastic
matrices are mixtures of permutation matrices, and trace estimates for
compositions of channels between rectangular matrix algebras.
