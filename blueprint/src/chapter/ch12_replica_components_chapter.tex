\chapter{Replica components, restoring operators and good-copy densities}
\label{ch:replica_components}

The replica argument of~\cite[Section~7]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} works with $k$
copies of a physical system and an auxiliary register. On each copy the ground
vector of the Hamiltonian splits the space into the ground direction and its
orthogonal complement; a subset $B$ of the copies labels the component in which
exactly the copies in $B$ are excited. The copies outside $B$ are the good
copies. This chapter proves the properties of these components, of the reduced
densities on the good copies, of the moments that arise when groups of copies
are merged, and of the restoring operators built from the eigenvectors of a
marginal.
