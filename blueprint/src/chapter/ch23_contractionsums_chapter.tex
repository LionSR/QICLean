\chapter{Sums of positive contractions and low-energy residuals}
\label{ch:contraction_sums}

This chapter records two estimates for a finite sum $H=\sum_i h_i$ of
matrices with $0\le h_i\le1$: every eigenvalue of $H$ lies between zero
and the number of terms, and the normalized powers of $1-H/N$ contract the
orthogonal complement of a gapped ground vector at the rate given by a lower
bound on the gap. A final section bounds the soft truncation of a Hermitian
matrix on the low spectral span of a commuting comparison operator plus a
perturbation whose imaginary-time conjugation is bounded.
