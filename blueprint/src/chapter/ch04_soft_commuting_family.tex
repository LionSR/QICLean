\section{Estimated energy centers and a common commuting interior}
\label{sec:soft_commuting_family}

The ten-unit interval of estimated regional energies in
Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite[lines 1178--1185]{Arad2016RigorousRG}
permits a soft replacement to have negative spectrum. A scalar correction
restores positivity. The same correction can be summed over a selected
family without requiring the resulting soft operators to be nearest-neighbour
sums. This is an order construction for the three regional replacements
in \cite[lines 1373--1392]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.

\begin{lemma}[One actual estimated-center replacement]
    \label{lem:soft_estimated_center_order}
    \lean{Matrix.IsHermitian.softTruncation_estimated_center_bounds}
    \leanok
    Let $A=A^*\geq eI$, $e-10\leq p\leq e$, $t>0$ and $m\geq1$.
    Put
    \begin{align}
        F=pI+h_{m,t}(A-pI)-eI.
    \end{align}
    Then
    \begin{align}
        F+10I\geq0,\qquad F\leq A-eI.
    \end{align}
    Every matrix commuting with $A$ also commutes with $F$.
    For every $U\geq0$,
    \begin{align}
        \left(U-\frac{t}{m}\left(\frac Ut\right)^m\right)
        \mathbf1_{[p+U,\infty)}(A)\leq F+10I.
    \end{align}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    The matrix $X=A-pI$ is positive. Its soft function is positive and
    lies below $X$. Moreover,
    \begin{align}
        F+10I=h_{m,t}(X)+(p-e+10)I,
    \end{align}
    whose scalar correction is nonnegative. This proves positivity
    and transfers the actual high-energy floor from $h_{m,t}(X)$.
    The upper bound follows from $h_{m,t}(X)\leq X$.
    Scalar translation in the functional calculus expresses the soft
    term as a function of $A$, which proves commutant preservation.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous estimated-center replacements]
    \label{lem:soft_commuting_family_order}
    \lean{Matrix.softTruncation_commuting_family_bounds}
    \leanok
    Let $(A_i)_{i\in\mathcal I}$ be a finite pairwise commuting
    Hermitian family, with $A_i\geq e_iI$, and let
    $S\subseteq\mathcal I$. For $i\in S$ suppose
    $e_i-10\leq p_i\leq e_i$. For $t>0$ and $m\geq1$, construct
    \begin{align}
        F_i & =
        \begin{cases}
            p_iI+h_{m,t}(A_i-p_iI)-e_iI, & i\in S,    \\
            A_i-e_iI,                    & i\notin S,
        \end{cases} \\
        B   & =\sum_i F_i,\qquad D=10|S|.
    \end{align}
    Then
    \begin{align}
        B+DI & \geq0, & B & \leq\sum_i(A_i-e_iI),
    \end{align}
    and $B$ commutes with every original $A_j$. For each selected
    $j\in S$ and every $U\geq0$,
    \begin{align}
        \left(U-\frac{t}{m}\left(\frac Ut\right)^m\right)
        \mathbf1_{[p_j+U,\infty)}(A_j)\leq B+DI.
    \end{align}
    The family and the selected set may be empty, and the matrix space
    may have dimension zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Put $K_i=F_i+10I$ for $i\in S$ and $K_i=F_i$ otherwise.
    Lemma~\ref{lem:soft_estimated_center_order} and the lower regional
    bounds give $K_i\geq0$. Their literal sum is $B+DI$.
    Sum the individual upper bounds to obtain the upper order estimate.
    Commutant preservation gives simultaneous commutation with every
    original regional matrix. For the selected projection estimate,
    use its derived floor below $K_j$, followed by
    $K_j\leq\sum_iK_i$.
\end{proof}

These statements construct the common interior matrix after several
soft replacements. A physical application must still derive the
regional lower energies and pairwise commutation, and construct the
actual boundary forcing and ground-energy bounds. No reverse residual,
spectral gap, matrix product representation or complete efficient-filter
theorem is supplied by these generic order results.
