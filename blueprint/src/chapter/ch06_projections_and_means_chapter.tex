\chapter{Projections, compressions and matrix means}
\label{ch:projections_and_means}

isometric coordinates, and for the finite trees of weighted geometric means of
positive matrices introduced in Chapter~\ref{ch:operator_convexity}. They
supply the comparison steps in the metric estimates
of~\cite[Section~8]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} and in several estimates
of~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}.
