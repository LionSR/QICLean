\chapter{Monomial operators, Gauss operators, and finite-group commutants}
\label{ch:monomial_operators}

This chapter collects finite-dimensional facts about monomial matrices, which
send each standard basis vector to a multiple of another one, and about
unitary representations of finite groups. The common fixed space of a family
of involutive monomial operators along the fibers of a hypercube is counted
by the fibers on which the phases have trivial holonomy. The local Gauss
operators built from a family of unitary operators indexed by pairs of group
elements form a unitary representation, whose normalized average is an
orthogonal projection. Finally, every unital matrix star-subalgebra is the
commutant of a unitary representation of a finite group, built from signed
permutation matrices.
